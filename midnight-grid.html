    W A S D or arrows. A turn eases in while you hold it, and eases back to straight when you let go. The steer slider sets how quickly that happens. Space boost. Shift Ludicrous. Q Autopilot. E Full Self-Driving. F, the EMP button, or the right bumper fires your one shot. You start with one. The purple rings on the road reload it, and only when the EMP bar is empty. The body bar shrinks with each crash, and the car slows until it is limping. A hit spins the other car to a stop. The lap menu sets how long the race is. Free roam has no finish line and no other cars, and it runs until you quit. Novice, Medium, and Expert change the other cars and the course. In Free roam they change the course. The tunnel gives every car a speed boost. Missing the tunnel and going around it keeps you on the surface. Yellow arrows point the right way around the track. The Roadster can steer up the tunnel walls and across the ceiling. T, or the left bumper, is the Roadster's Cold Gas Thrusters: a short hop, then the gold bar has to refill. You start looking out the windshield. C cycles the outside cameras, then back to the cockpit. Esc or P pauses the race and the music. The finish keeps that song playing until you quit or start another race. Quit returns to this board. R reset. M mute. Gamepad: triggers, stick, A boost, Y Ludicrous, X Autopilot, B Full Self-Driving, right bumper EMP, left bumper thrusters, Start pauses.
