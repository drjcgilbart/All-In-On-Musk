# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Friday, 20 Nov 2026 · Issue 009 · Times ET**

## The brief

- Tesla's two-seat Cybercab, with no steering wheel or pedals, opened to public riders in Dallas on Thursday. It's the second city after Austin.
- SpaceX says the booster from Starship Flight 15 aborted its catch safely, and that very little debris has turned up on Mexico's coast.
- The Boring Company signed a 40-year deal for a Music City Loop station 22 feet from the terminal entrance at Nashville's airport.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. "Howdy Dallas." Cybercab gets a second city.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · Robotaxi · Cybercab · 19 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Tesla's Robotaxi account at 11:47 AM ET Thursday: "Howdy Dallas. Cybercab has arrived in your city." Tesla's main account followed at 3:02 PM ET: "Cybercab expands to second city." Public rides in the two-seat car, which has no steering wheel or pedals, had been limited to Austin since September.

**Reported:** Teslarati says riders book through the Tesla Robotaxi app, and that unsupervised Model Y Robotaxis have run in Dallas since April. InsideEVs notes Cybercab is only in Austin and Dallas so far, while the other Robotaxi cities run Model Ys. Tesla's AI chief Ashok Elluswamy had said on the October earnings call that Dallas would be next.

- **The fleet:** Teslarati reported Wednesday that Tesla added a record 200 Cybercabs to the Texas driverless-vehicle register on 17 Nov, putting it at 773 Cybercabs and 1,507 vehicles total, ahead of Waymo's 1,321. Registration doesn't mean a car is carrying riders.
- **For buyers:** Personal Cybercab orders opened last week at $29,990 with a required $299-a-month Autonomy subscription. Deliveries are targeted for December.

> Read it as: the launch is on the record. Tesla hasn't said how many Cybercabs are running in Dallas or how big the service area is.

Sources: [Robotaxi on X](https://x.com/robotaxi/status/2123458263522107564) · [Tesla on X](https://x.com/Tesla/status/2123507319040528546) · [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-cybercab-without-steering-wheel-pedals-expands-public-rides-dallas/) · [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/816139/tesla-cybercab-robotaxi-expands-to-dallas/) · [Teslarati, register](https://www.teslarati.com/teslas-cybercab-fleet-just-had-its-biggest-day-ever/)

### 02. SpaceX on the Mexico debris: "very little," and not clearly ours.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Starship · Mexico · 17–19 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** SpaceX at 3:38 PM ET Thursday: on Flight 15, the Super Heavy booster "correctly performed a safe abort when returning for a catch" and splashed down off Starbase. When reports came in of debris in Mexican territory, SpaceX says it offered specialists to help identify it and clean up. Partners on the ground "have found very little debris," and what they found is "not clearly from SpaceX operations."

SpaceX called most social posts claiming widespread debris and damage "outright fabrications or intentionally misleading," and repeated that independent tests found the rocket's materials pose no chemical, biological or toxicological risk.

**The other side:** Mexico's environmental agency, Profepa, posted on 17 Nov that inspectors were at Playa Bagdad in Tamaulipas to identify material that began reaching Mexican waters that morning. It said the material would be secured and that it would ask the company to take responsibility for it. SpaceX's statement quotes that post. Neither side has said what the material is.

Sources: [SpaceX on X](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2123516375805379040) · [Profepa on X](https://x.com/PROFEPA_Mx/status/2122753423837978698) · [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/19/spacex-starship-flight-15-debris-mexico/)

### 03. USSF-317 scrubs. Dragon tries to leave today.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Falcon 9 · Dragon · 19–21 Nov 2026

SpaceX scrubbed Thursday night's Falcon 9 launch of the classified USSF-317 mission from Vandenberg and didn't say why. The new target is tonight, Friday, in a four-hour window opening at 9:32 PM ET, with a backup Saturday. Booster B1100 is flying for the 11th time and will land on the droneship Of Course I Still Love You.

The CRS-35 cargo Dragon is still set to undock from the space station no earlier than 12:05 PM ET today, with NASA coverage from 11:45 AM ET. NASA's Thursday update moves splashdown off California to about 4:55 PM ET Saturday.

Sources: [Spaceflight Now](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/19/live-coverage-spacex-to-launch-classified-payload-for-the-u-s-space-force-2/) · [SpaceX: USSF-317](https://www.spacex.com/launches/ussf317) · [NASA advisory](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-to-cover-35th-spacex-resupply-mission-space-station-departure/)

### 04. A Loop station 22 feet from the terminal door.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Boring · Nashville · 18–19 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** The Boring Company at 5:06 PM ET Thursday said it and the Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority "have signed a 40-year agreement for a Music City Loop station," with trips of "less than 10 minutes from downtown" to a station "22 feet from the terminal entrance." Musk at 10:00 PM ET: "Cool."

**Reported:** Aviation Pros says the deal was signed 18 Nov. Boring pays all construction, operating and maintenance costs, plus a $300,000 yearly license fee rising 3% a year and $5 per passenger pickup or drop-off. Tunnel portals would sit behind Concourse D. Two boring machines are digging under downtown and a third is being prepared. Boring is aiming for early 2027, with no firm opening date.

Sources: [Boring on X](https://x.com/boringcompany/status/2123538445918224762) · [Aviation Pros](https://www.aviationpros.com/airport-business/airport-infrastructure-operations/news/55420211/nashville-international-airport-finalizes-tunnel-agreement-with-the-boring-company) · [WSMV](https://www.wsmv.com/2026/11/20/boring-company-says-it-signed-40-year-agreement-music-city-loop-station-nashville-airport/) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2123612464462082292)

### 05. Musk at Giga Shanghai: more output. Waitlists hit 300 Superchargers.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · Shanghai · Charging · Energy · 19 Nov 2026

**Reported:** Reuters, citing a social media account tied to Chinese state broadcaster CCTV, says Musk told trade official Ren Hongbin on Thursday that Tesla will keep increasing output at its China factory. Reuters says it was Musk's first visit to the Shanghai plant since 2023. The plant delivered 810,889 Model 3s and Model Ys in the first 10 months of this year, up 21.4%.

**Confirmed:** Tesla Charging at 5:29 PM ET: Waitlist "is now live at 300 Supercharger sites across the US," joinable in the Tesla app or the car's screen, including for non-Teslas. Tesla Energy at 11:04 AM ET: the three-phase Powerwall 3P can now be ordered in Switzerland. Tesla is also still pushing shareholder votes ahead of its 3 Dec annual meeting.

Sources: [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/musk-pledges-higher-tesla-output-at-china-plant-chinese-state-media-says-5037774) · [Tesla Charging on X](https://x.com/TeslaCharging/status/2123544093213274167) · [Tesla Energy on X](https://x.com/teslaenergy/status/2123447290044264567) · [Tesla on X, vote](https://x.com/Tesla/status/2123438650000330910)

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. A 266,000-square-foot logistics hub for KSC.
`Confirmed` · Space · Kennedy Space Center · 19 Nov 2026

All Points Logistics named Expeditors as the exclusive logistics provider for its planned Space Prep Spaceport Logistics Center on a 65-acre site at Kennedy Space Center, within five miles of the pads. The 266,000-square-foot building is due to open by the end of 2027, next to a planned 260,000-square-foot spacecraft processing center. It will serve operators at KSC and Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.

Sources: [Space Coast Daily](https://spacecoastdaily.com/2026/11/brevard-based-all-points-announces-partnership-with-expeditors-for-space-prep-logistics-center-at-kennedy-space-center/)

### 02. Floods close the Visitor Complex through today.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Space Coast · Weather · 19–20 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex closed Thursday and stays closed today, Friday, because of heavy rain and flooding. It plans to reopen at 9 AM Saturday, weather permitting. **Reported:** AP says Brevard County saw nearly 100 high-water rescues Thursday, and the St. Johns River near Cocoa rose above a record set in 1953. Flash flooding was expected to last through Friday.

Sources: [KSC Visitor Complex via Cape Coral Breeze](https://www.capecoralbreeze.com/news/florida-news-apwire/2026/11/19/kennedy-space-center-visitor-complex-to-remain-closed-through-friday-nov-20-due-to-weather/) · [AP via WJTV](https://www.wjtv.com/weather/severe-weather/ap-soaking-storms-shift-north-to-floridas-space-coast-and-prompts-water-rescues-of-humans-and-horses/)

### 03. Anthropic's record IPO closes up 17%.
`Confirmed` · AI · Markets · 18–19 Nov 2026

Anthropic priced at $83 a share and raised $81.3 billion, the biggest IPO on record and larger than SpaceX's June debut, CNBC says.
