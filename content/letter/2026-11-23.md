# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Monday, 23 Nov 2026 · Issue 012 · Times ET**

## The brief

- Musk says Tesla's next chip, AI5, goes to Optimus first and cars stay on AI4, which he says is "enough to solve superhuman driving." He also cut the interim AI4.5 board's memory from 32GB to 24GB because "RAM is super expensive."
- Built-in Starlink for Teslas beyond the Cybercab is "hopefully available towards the end of next year," Musk says, except in China.
- SpaceX launched NROL-136 from California 32 hours after its last launch from the same pad, its fastest turnaround yet. The next Cape launch is NG-25 on Sunday, 29 Nov.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. AI5 goes to Optimus. Cars stay on AI4.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · AI5 · Optimus · AI4.5 · 22 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Asked by an owner with an older AI3 car when AI5 would be in new cars, Musk replied at 7:09 AM ET Sunday: "It's going to Optimus. Cars will remain at AI4. That's enough to solve superhuman driving." Earlier, explaining that the new FSD v15 Reality View runs on half of one of three TRIP accelerators on the car's second chip, he wrote: "AI5 is a gigantic improvement. Roughly 12X more compute." At 2:09 PM ET he put it at "roughly an order of magnitude hardware performance improvement, all things considered," adding that memory bandwidth is a constraint.

On the in-between board: "AI4.5 is a relatively small performance improvement that is mostly due to needing to upgrade RAM to LPDDR5X" (2:26 PM ET), and "RAM is super expensive, so we reduced AI4.5 from 32GB to 24GB. That will be fine" (5:31 PM ET). At 7:06 AM ET he said "AI4 costs twice as much now due to memory and storage price hikes."

**Also:** Musk at 6:35 AM ET on Optimus: "It will be our finest product ever and (I think) the best product of all time of any kind." At 3:52 PM ET, quoting a post about Tesla's world model being shared between Autopilot and Optimus: "Tesla super intelligence software, chip design & Terafab are fun places to work."

**Reported:** Teslarati notes the 24GB figure is down from the 32GB Musk described on Tesla's first-quarter call. TeslaNorth says AI3 cars run a separate FSD v14 Lite branch, not v15, and Musk gave no date for the upgrade he has promised AI3 owners who bought FSD.

> Read it as: no AI5 car is coming any time soon, by Musk's own word. Tesla hasn't said which cars get AI4.5 or when, and the 12X and "order of magnitude" figures are his estimates, not published specs.

Sources: [Musk on X, Optimus first](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124475458150334900) · [Musk on X, 12X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124459444620628193) · [Musk on X, order of magnitude](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124581034419499420) · [Musk on X, AI4.5](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124585347929767967) · [Musk on X, 24GB](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124631858340049214) · [Musk on X, AI4 cost](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124474606702473263) · [Musk on X, Optimus](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124466805263470612) · [Musk on X, Terafab](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124606900553863466) · [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-starlink-built-in-ai5/) · [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/22/tesla-ai4-ai5-optimus/)

### 02. Built-in Starlink for more Teslas, "towards the end of next year."
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · Starlink · 22 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Replying at 8:15 PM ET Sunday to David Marcus, who said he couldn't wait for an embedded Starlink option in all models beyond the Cybercab, Musk wrote: "Hopefully available towards the end of next year. This will be important for unsupervised FSD, so passengers have high bandwidth everywhere for work and entertainment." At 8:37 PM ET he added: "Except in China, as Starlink is illegal there."

**Reported:** Teslarati says the Cybercab is the only Tesla with Starlink today, a flush-mounted terminal on the rear hatch, and riders don't yet appear to be able to connect to it. It says Musk gave no price for adding Starlink to other cars.

> Read it as: "hopefully" and "towards the end of next year" are not a ship date. No models, price or service plan have been announced.

Sources: [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124673053246312848) · [Musk on X, China](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124678745629790551) · [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-starlink-built-in-ai5/)

### 03. 32 hours, same pad. A new record.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Falcon 9 · NROL-136 · 22 Nov 2026

Falcon 9 launched NROL-136 for the National Reconnaissance Office from pad 4E at Vandenberg at 7:23 AM ET Sunday. SpaceX: it lifted off "32 hours after teams launched USSF-317, completing our fastest turnaround between launches from the same pad." SpaceX launch VP Kiko Dontchev put it at "32 hr, 3 min, 21 sec," beating the previous record of 45 hours from pad 40 in Florida. "Next goal… the 24 hr turn!" Musk at 2:42 PM ET: "32 hour launch pad recycle. Great work by SpaceX team."

The booster, on its 14th flight, landed at Landing Zone 4. The NRO says it was its fourth launch this year for its proliferated constellation. Spaceflight Now counts it as the 680th Falcon booster landing.

**Next from the Cape:** Falcon 9 is set to launch Northrop Grumman's Cygnus XL cargo ship (NG-25) to the space station from pad 40 on Sunday, 29 Nov, at 10:50 AM ET, with a backup on Monday, 30 Nov, at 10:24 AM ET. The booster is set to land at LZ-40.

**Also:** Musk at 3:59 PM ET, quoting a post about Starship's upper stage catch from orbit last week: "This was done without AI/SI, but we will be using a lot of SI in the future."

Sources: [SpaceX on X](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2124576203113636183) · [Dontchev on X](https://x.com/TurkeyBeaver/status/2124584686626484446) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124589255565680900) · [SpaceX, NROL-136](https://www.spacex.com/launches/nrol136) · [NRO](https://www.nro.gov/news-media-featured-stories/news-media-press-releases/Archived-Press-Releases/Press-Release-Article/Article/4655832/nro-successfully-launches-nrol-136-november-22/) · [Spaceflight Now](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/21/live-coverage-spacex-to-launch-intelligence-gathering-satellites-for-the-national-reconnaissance-office-3/) · [SpaceX, NG-25](https://www.spacex.com/launches/ng25) · [Musk on X, Starship](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124608788158447644)

### 04. Texas register: Tesla 1,507, Waymo 1,364.
`Reported / Confirmed` · Tesla · Robotaxi · Cybercab · Texas · 22–23 Nov 2026

**Reported:** EVwire's tracker of the Texas DMV automated-vehicle register, checked at 2:07 AM ET today, lists 1,507 vehicles under Tesla Robotaxi (773 Cybercabs and 734 Model Ys) against 1,364 for Waymo, a lead of 143. The gap was 162 until Waymo added 19 Ojai vans on Friday. Most of Tesla's jump came from one batch of 200 Cybercabs on 17 Nov.

**Confirmed:** Musk at 4:44 PM ET Sunday, on a post saying some Dallas riders now prefer the Cybercab to discounted rivals: "Most people don't yet realize that Cybercab would win even at price parity and its cost will be much less."

> Read it as: registered means allowed to run driverless in Texas, not giving rides. The register lists VINs, not cities, and nobody publishes how many cars are in service at a given moment.

Sources: [EVwire tracker](https://tracker.evwire.com/) · [EVwire, 17 Nov batch](https://evwire.com/p/tesla-200-cybercabs-texas-robotaxi-lead-waymo-186) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124619934080836039)

### 06. A 6-gigawatt campus, and a Dec. 9 vote.
`Confirmed / Reported` · SpaceX · Data centers · Arizona · 22 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** SpaceX's own project page for Cochise County, Arizona, describes a supercomputing campus of nine buildings with up to six gigawatts of power and up to 24 gigawatt-hours of battery storage, dry-cooled "with no industrial water consumption," to "train SpaceX's super intelligence models." Musk reposted a summary of the plan at 7:03 AM ET Sunday.

**Reported:** Vox Luci reported Sunday that the Cochise County Planning and Zoning Commission takes up the permit, filed by SpaceX affiliate Tombstone Resources, at 2 PM on Wednesday, 9 Dec, in Bisbee. Arizona Attorney General Kris Mayes asked the commission on 19 Nov to wait until state and federal reviews are done. 12News says six gigawatts is close to 20% of Arizona's current generating capacity.

> Read it as: this is a proposal. The jobs, tax and power-bill figures come from the application, and the county says it hasn't verified them.

Sources: [SpaceX, Cochise County](https://www.spacex.com/sites/cochise-county) · [Vox Luci](https://voxluci.com/local/cities-and-counties/spacex-cochise-county-data-center-vote/) · [12News](https://www.12news.com/article/news/local/arizona/spacexs-massive-proposed-data-center-southeastern-arizona-wilcox-cochise-county/75-3c7dcd2c-a864-4777-8bf1-951cef11a399) · [Post Musk reposted](https://x.com/XFreeze/status/2124203543242719564)

## Outside the core: Beyond the Stack

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. The St. Johns crests above the record. A slow decline next.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Space Coast · Weather · 22–23 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** NWS Melbourne at 9:21 PM Sunday: the St. Johns River near Cocoa read 18.7 feet at 8:15 PM, with a 24-hour high of 18.8, and "the crest has exceeded the previous flood of record." Flood stage there is 16 feet. The river "is expected to begin a very slow decline through Major Flood Stage this week." Sunday morning, Florida 511 still listed flood closures on parts of SR A1A and SR 50 in Brevard, and rain chances return Tuesday night into Wednesday. **Reported:** WESH says parts of the area near Cocoa were still underwater Sunday night, and the Brevard sheriff told the station it could take weeks for all the water to recede. Never drive through floodwater.

Sources: [NWS/NOAA Cocoa gauge](https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/cocf1) · [Space Coast Wire](https://spacecoastwire.com/briefings/2026-11-22-daily) · [WESH](https://www.wesh.com/article/brevard-county-floodwater-sits-neighborhoods-near-cocoa/74501998)

### 02. OpenAI publishes its pretraining curve.
`Confirmed` · AI · Safety · 22 Nov 2026

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki posted "Perplexity and Intelligence" at 12:03 PM ET Sunday. For the first time it publishes OpenAI's internal pretraining scores, how well each base model predicts held-out text from OpenAI's own codebase, from about 0.99 for GPT-3.5 to about 0.91 for GPT-4, and below 0.8 for its newest models. He proposes common limits across labs based on perplexity and other measures, "with progress beyond them gated on our ability to understand AI behavior," but sets no number. Musk replied at 2:41 PM ET that pretraining "should just be called 'training'," and at 3:48 PM ET: "Imagine what super intelligence will be like when we hit an exawatt."

Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence) · [Pachocki on X](https://x.com/merettm/status/2124549218995949967) · [Musk on X, training](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124588982117781808) · [Musk on X, exawatt](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124605881770033304)
