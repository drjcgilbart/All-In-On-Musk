# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Sunday, 22 Nov 2026 · Issue 011 · Times ET**

## The brief

- Tesla started rolling out FSD (Supervised) v15 on Saturday afternoon, claiming "25x better handling of safety critical cases" and a new live Reality View. Musk says wide release is next month.
- The Boring Company says its third Nashville tunnel machine, Prufrock-MB3, is "alive" and will be tunneling by the end of the month. A fourth arrives in December.
- Dragon splashed down off Los Angeles Saturday, ending CRS-35. Falcon 9 is set to launch NROL-136 from California at 7:23 AM ET today.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. FSD v15 starts rolling out.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · FSD v15 · 21 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Tesla at 2:24 PM ET Saturday: "FSD v15 now starting to roll out – upgrading every part of the end-to-end driving network." Its headline claim is "25x better handling of safety critical cases." The post also lists better detection of signs, animals, debris, potholes and standing water, more natural speed in school zones and bad weather, slowing for speed cameras, and a Bike Rack Mode for cars with hitch accessories. Start-and-end-of-trip navigation now learns from fleet route preferences, and Tesla says later v15 releases will extend that to the whole route.

Tesla AI chief Ashok Elluswamy called it the "first wave of customers today." The release notes, posted by the Tesla Super Intelligence account, credit the 25x figure to a vision encoder shared by FSD, Robotaxi and Optimus and trained on "millions of internet videos." They also say driver monitoring now works more often with hats and sunglasses and is less strict in higher-speed driver profiles. Musk at 7:11 PM ET: "Tesla FSD V15 is a banger release!"

**Reported:** Teslarati says the first builds are going to Early Access Program members. TeslaNorth says v15 is built for AI4 (HW4) cars, older HW3 cars stay on a separate branch, and Tesla still hasn't dated the HW3 upgrade it promised in April.

> Read it as: the 25x number is Tesla's own internal metric, and Tesla hasn't published the data behind it. It's still FSD (Supervised), so the driver has to pay attention and take over when needed. Wide release in December is Musk's word, with no date.

Sources: [Tesla on X](https://x.com/Tesla/status/2124222447855743019) · [Release notes on X](https://x.com/TeslaSI/status/2124229673056113100) · [Elluswamy on X](https://x.com/aelluswamy/status/2124224924512641512) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124294755316310346) · [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-full-self-driving-v15-rollout-begins-full-release-notes-improvements/) · [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/21/tesla-fsd-v15-what-we-know/)

### 02. Reality View: the car draws the road.
`Confirmed` · Tesla · AI4 · Grok · 21 Nov 2026

Elluswamy: v15 "debuts the Tesla Reality View." The same end-to-end driving model also renders a realistic view of the world around the car, with arrows for the turns it plans to take and pins for where it plans to park. He said the new view uses less compute than the old visualization, and later called it "pixels-in-pixels-out."

Musk at 3:26 PM ET: the car "re-renders reality with chevrons in the sky for turns like a video game," and "you can tap any point on the reality revisualizer to make your car go there." At 5:35 PM ET he said it's "real-time AI video generation (no gaming engine!) using just half of one AI4 TRIP accelerator."

**Also:** Musk at 7:05 PM ET: "Tesla vehicles will share traffic knowledge and combine that with Grok's real-time 𝕏 feed," including "a heads up about what's happening at your destination."

> Read it as: the traffic-and-Grok feature is a plan, not part of v15. Musk didn't give a date or say which cars get it.

Sources: [Elluswamy on X, Reality View](https://x.com/aelluswamy/status/2124229978560835880) · [Elluswamy on X, compute](https://x.com/aelluswamy/status/2124230700547326435) · [Musk on X, chevrons](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124238164512714925) · [Musk on X, AI4](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124270426402836626) · [Musk on X, traffic](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124293036418990381) · [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/21/tesla-grok-live-traffic-destination-updates/)

### 03. Prufrock-MB3 is "alive" in Nashville.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Boring Company · Nashville · 21–22 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** The Boring Company at 1:55 AM ET today: "Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville!" It gets a week of commissioning and close-out, "and will be tunneling by end of month." ### 04. Dragon is home. NROL-136 flies this morning.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Dragon · Falcon 9 · 21–22 Nov 2026

SpaceX at 5:04 PM ET Saturday: "Splashdown of Dragon confirmed," off Southern California, ending its 35th cargo run to the space station. NASA says the capsule brought back a 3D-bioprinted blood vessel construct for heart research, frozen tumor organoids, microbe samples for studying antibiotic resistance, and a lighting system tested for astronaut sleep over three years.

Next up: Falcon 9 is set to launch NROL-136, a batch of National Reconnaissance Office satellites, from Vandenberg at 7:23 AM ET today. Spaceflight Now says booster B1097 is on its 14th flight and will try to land at Landing Zone 4.

**Also:** Musk at 9:13 PM ET Saturday, on a chart claiming SpaceX has put more mass in orbit in 20 years than the rest of the US combined since 1957: "Looks like an SI curve."

Sources: [SpaceX on X](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2124262638192660846) · [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/spacestation/2026/11/21/dragon-returns-to-earth-packed-with-science-and-cargo/) · [SpaceX, NROL-136](https://www.spacex.com/launches/nrol136) · [Spaceflight Now](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/21/live-coverage-spacex-to-launch-intelligence-gathering-satellites-for-the-national-reconnaissance-office-3/) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124325451908948451)

## Outside the core: Beyond the Stack

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. The St. Johns sets a record and stays there.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Weather · 21–22 Nov 2026

NWS Melbourne says the St. Johns River near Cocoa hit 18.8 feet Saturday afternoon, above the 17.55-foot record from 1953, and it "will set an all time flood of record." The river is forecast to stay steady into the middle of next week. The offshore low is no longer expected to turn tropical. A High Rip Current Risk runs through Sunday evening. Melbourne's November rain has reached 22.63 inches, against a normal of 1.77 by this date. Never drive through floodwater.

Sources: [Space Coast Wire](https://spacecoastwire.com/story/offshore-low-no-longer-tropical-brevard-flooding-nov-21)

### 02. An AI-assisted Riemann proof passes its checks.
`Confirmed / Reported` · AI · Math · 20–21 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** On Friday, mathematician Levent Alpöge released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, the 1859 problem about how prime numbers are spread out, as a Lean library of about 17 million lines. On Saturday the Lean and Mathlib communities said they "have full confidence" it's correct: it compiles, proves exactly Mathlib's statement of the hypothesis, and passes several independent proof checkers. **Reported:** The Guardian says Alpöge, who works at Anthropic, credits the company's Claude with helping develop the solution over about six weeks. Musk at 1:49 PM ET Saturday called it "yet another mind-blowing thing achieved by super intelligence."

Sources: [Lean community](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/) · [MIT verification files](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/) · [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/21/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-solved-claude-ai) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124213680422387741)
