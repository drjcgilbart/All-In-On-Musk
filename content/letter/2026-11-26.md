# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Thursday, 26 Nov 2026 · Issue 015 · Times ET**

## The brief

- SpaceX will give one newly issued share to every active Trump Account, and Musk says he'll match it with one of his own: "Every child in America will receive two SpaceX shares." SpaceX's share is due next month; his comes after his lockup ends in June 2027.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Two SpaceX shares for every kid in America, Musk says.
`Confirmed / Reported` · SpaceX · Musk · Trump Accounts · 25–26 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** SpaceX said Wednesday it will contribute one newly issued Class A share to every currently active Trump Account, the tax-advantaged savings accounts for U.S. children under 18, and that Musk will personally match the number of shares SpaceX donates. "We believe ownership of the company creating these systems should extend to the next generation of Americans," the company posted at 2:21 PM ET. The release says the plan depends on third-party and government approvals.

**Confirmed:** Musk at 10:43 PM ET Wednesday: "Every child in America will receive two SpaceX shares, one from the company and one from me personally." And at 1:01 AM ET today: "The share from SpaceX will be transferred next month. The share directly from me will be transferred when my lockup expires in June next year."

**Reported:** Reuters, via The Guardian, puts the two-share pledge at almost $18 billion, using Wednesday's $127.44 close and the nearly 70 million accounts the White House says exist. CNBC notes it's unclear how many accounts count as active, and that under Treasury rules families can't refuse gifted stock and generally can't sell it for five years or until the year the child turns 17, whichever comes first. Separately, Gwynne Shotwell's earlier gift of more than 2 million shares is due in December.

> Read it as: SpaceX's own share is the firm part, with a December target. Musk's personal share waits for June 2027. Dollar values move with the stock, and "active account" hasn't been defined yet.

Sources: [SpaceX investor release](https://ir.spacex.com/updates/releases-details/2026/SpaceX-to-Invest-in-the-Next-Generation-of-Americans-2026-r_LYQ5cH94/default.aspx) · [SpaceX on X](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2125671356339499494) · [Musk on X, pledge](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2125797463047258119) · [Musk on X, timing](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2125832205301878841) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/spacex-musk-trump-account-stock.html) · [The Guardian (Reuters)](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/26/elon-musk-pledges-to-give-two-spacex-shares-to-every-child-in-the-us)

### 02. FDA clears Neuralink's first pivotal trial.
`Confirmed` · Neuralink · FDA · ALS · 25 Nov 2026

Neuralink said at 3:54 PM ET Wednesday that the FDA approved the LINK Pivotal Study, the first pivotal trial for an implantable brain-computer interface in the U.S. It will test safety and effectiveness in people with severe paralysis from ALS, measuring how well they can control digital devices by thought. The company says more than 30 people have been implanted in its earlier feasibility studies in the U.S., Canada and the UK. Enrollment is open.

Neuralink president DJ Seo at 7:24 PM ET: 20 participants, primary endpoints judged at 90 days, and a premarket approval filing planned once the 20th participant reaches them. "Takes months for FDA to review the submission."

> Read it as: approval to run the trial, not to sell the implant. Sites and a ClinicalTrials.gov listing haven't been published yet.

Sources: [Neuralink](https://neuralink.com/updates/neuralink-receives-fda-approval-to-launch-link-pivotal-study/) · [Neuralink trials page](https://neuralink.com/trials/device-control/) · [Neuralink on X](https://x.com/neuralink/status/2125694580544680185) · [DJ Seo on X](https://x.com/djseo/status/2125747376782532906) · [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/neuralink-receives-fda-approval-launch-pivotal-brain-implant-trial-als)

### 03. FCC clears a million data-center satellites.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Starmind · FCC · AI compute · 25 Nov 2026

FCC Chairman Brendan Carr at 11:09 AM ET Wednesday: "Today, in a historic first, the FCC approved SpaceX's request for 1 million satellites (orbital compute modules) to create data centers in space." The Space Bureau's order covers the Starmind constellation in shells from 500 to 1,002 km, with a 15-year term. Via Satellite reports the first satellite must launch by 25 November 2033. The order was granted in part and deferred in part, with conditions, and the radio spectrum it covers is for backup command links if the satellites' laser links fail.

> Read it as: a license, not hardware in orbit. SpaceX hasn't said when the first Starmind satellite will fly.

Sources: [FCC order (PDF)](https://eu-assets.contentstack.com/v3/assets/blt23eb5bbc4124baa6/bltf4cdf2af47159e12/6b071c5f5506bb38bca5b0ec/FCC_report_and_order_on_SpaceX_Starmind_AI_constellation_11-25-2026.pdf) · [Carr on X](https://x.com/BrendanCarrFCC/status/2125623026918002860) · [Via Satellite](https://www.satellitetoday.com/connectivity/2026/11/25/fcc-approves-spacexs-1-million-satellite-orbital-data-center-constellation/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/spacex-fcc-satellite-approval.html)

### 04. Tesla's Dallas Robotaxi zone nearly doubles.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · Robotaxi · Dallas · 25 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Tesla's Robotaxi account at 1:28 PM ET Wednesday: "Just grew wider in Dallas. More cars, less $, Happy Thanksgiving!" with a new service map. Tesla gave no fleet count or fare figures.

**Reported:** EVwire and Drive Tesla, tracing the maps, put the zone at about 150 square miles, up roughly 83% from 82. Most of the new ground is north of I-635, toward Farmers Branch, Carrollton, Addison and Richardson. Cybercab rides started in Dallas about a week ago.

> Read it as: the square mileage is outside estimates from Tesla's map. Check both ends of a trip in the app.

Sources: [Robotaxi on X](https://x.com/robotaxi/status/2125657961242726732) · [EVwire](https://evwire.com/p/tesla-robotaxi-dallas-geofence-expansion-150-square-miles) · [Drive Tesla](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-robotaxi-dallas-expansion/)

### 05. SpaceX asks Arizona county to keep its Dec. 9 vote.
`Confirmed / Reported` · SpaceX · Arizona · AI compute · 24–25 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** SpaceX at 12:07 PM ET Wednesday said its supercompute site in Cochise County, Arizona, has "no gas plant, no industrial water use, and a dry-cooling closed-loop water system," and that it will invest $50 billion-plus, with 1,000 permanent jobs and up to 5,500 construction jobs.

**Reported:** The Tucson Daily Brief says a 24 November SpaceX letter asks the county Planning and Zoning Commission to keep its 9 December hearing rather than delay it, as Arizona Attorney General Kris Mayes requested, and states that the applicant, Tombstone Resources LLC, is a wholly owned SpaceX subsidiary. Mayes' office says questions remain about power: up to 6 gigawatts at full build-out.

> Read it as: the county vote is a land-use permit. Power service still needs state utility approval.

Sources: [SpaceX on X](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2125637438710153247) · [Tucson Daily Brief](https://tucsondailybrief.com/news-reports/cochise-spacex-letter-2026-11-25.html) · [AZPM](https://www.azpm.org/datacenters/2026-11-23/ag-mayes-asks-cochise-county-officials-to-delay-vote-on-planned-spacex-data-center-permit)

### 08. FSD (Supervised) passes 18 billion miles.
`Confirmed` · Tesla · FSD · 24–26 Nov 2026

## Outside the core: Beyond the Stack

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. Blue Origin books two more New Glenn flights from the Cape.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Launch · 25 Nov 2026

Blue Origin said Wednesday that satellite builder EnduroSat bought two dedicated New Glenn launches from Cape Canaveral, the first in 2029. Via Satellite notes it's New Glenn's second deal in a week, and that the rocket is grounded while Blue Origin rebuilds its pad after a summer test-stand explosion; a return to flight is reportedly expected in early 2027.

Sources: [Blue Origin](https://www.blueorigin.com/news/endurosat-signs-multi-launch-agreement) · [Via Satellite](https://www.satellitetoday.com/launch/2026/11/25/endurosat-buys-2-dedicated-launches-from-blue-origin/)

### 02. The St. Johns at Cocoa: still above flood stage.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Weather · 26 Nov 2026

The NOAA gauge on the St. Johns River near Cocoa read 18.39 feet at 2:15 AM ET today, down a few hundredths from yesterday's 18.42 and still above the 17-foot major flood mark (flood stage is 16). Check Florida 511 before driving in Brevard, and never drive through floodwater.

Sources: [NWS/NOAA Cocoa gauge](https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/cocf1)

### 03. Waymo recalls nearly 4,500 robotaxis over closed freeway lanes.
`Confirmed` · Robotaxis · Competition · 24 Nov 2026

Waymo told NHTSA its recall of nearly 4,500 U.S. vehicles followed 11 incidents since 1 October of robotaxis entering freeway lanes closed for construction, three of them on 9 November, CNBC reports. No collisions or injuries were reported, and the fix is still in the works. Waymo has halted U.S. freeway operations after a California ban.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/waymo-recall-prompted-by-robotaxis-entering-closed-freeway-lanes.html)
