# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Friday, 27 Nov 2026 · Issue 016 · Times ET**

## The brief

- Musk on Thanksgiving: "I will be donating another share of SpaceX to all kids in America every year." That's on top of this week's two-share pledge. No start date or mechanics yet. SpaceX stock rose about 7.5% in overnight trading, CNBC reports.
- The EU's motor-vehicle committee meets on 15 December, and for the first time the draft agenda lists the Tesla Assisted Driving file "with possible vote".
- Tesla added a record 285 Cybercabs to its Texas robotaxi register on Thanksgiving, taking the Cybercab count past 1,000, to 1,107.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Another SpaceX share for every kid, every year.
`Confirmed / Reported` · SpaceX · Musk · Trump Accounts · 26–27 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Musk at 4:24 PM ET Thursday, replying on X: "I will be donating another share of SpaceX to all kids in America every year." It builds on Wednesday's plan, covered here yesterday: one new share from SpaceX to every active Trump Account next month, and one from Musk when his lockup ends in June 2027. At 7:26 PM ET, quoting a post about the pledge, he added: "Vital that children have a stake in the future."

**Reported:** CNBC says SpaceX shares rose 7.47% overnight on the Blue Ocean ATS platform after the post. U.S. exchanges were shut Thursday and close early today. Reuters notes Musk said last month the first share would be "the first of many."

> Read it as: a pledge in a reply, not a filing. There's no start year, no account rules and no word on whether the yearly share goes through Trump Accounts. Overnight prices are thin and can reverse when regular trading opens.

Sources: [Musk on X, every year](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126064597039997254) · [Musk on X, stake](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126110344120762593) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/spacex-shares-musk-donations.html) · [Reuters via Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/article-musk-pledges-two-spacex-shares-for-every-us-child-in-18-billion-ai/)

### 02. EU puts Tesla's assisted driving up for a possible vote.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · Europe · Assisted Driving · 24–26 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** The European Commission's draft agenda for the 120th meeting of the Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles, dated 24 November, lists item 4 for 15 December, 10:40 to 11:10 Brussels time (4:40 to 5:10 AM ET): "Further examination on draft Commission implementing decision authorising the Kingdom of the Netherlands to grant EU type-approval for the 'Tesla Assisted Driving' system (with possible vote)."

**Reported:** Reuters says it's the first time the Commission has explicitly raised a vote. EU-wide approval needs at least 15 of the 27 member states, representing 65% of the EU's population. Nearly a dozen countries already follow the Dutch approval, and CNBC notes a rejection at EU level would invalidate those national approvals.

> Read it as: a vote is possible, not scheduled for certain, and the agenda is still a draft. Tesla Assisted Driving is the EU name for FSD (Supervised).

Sources: [TCMV draft agenda (PDF)](https://circabc.europa.eu/sd/a/cd7afd62-f795-4439-b14f-f7271f3fbd57/Draft%20Agenda%20TCMV%2015.12.2026.pdf) · [Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/27/eu-may-vote-on-authorising-tesla-assisted-driving-system-at-dec-15-meeting) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/eu-postpones-discussion-to-approve-teslas-self-driving-tech.html)

### 03. A record 285 Cybercabs join Tesla's Texas register.
`Reported` · Tesla · Cybercab · Texas · 26 Nov 2026

Tesla Robotaxi LLC's entry on the Texas DMV's automated-vehicle register grew by 285 vehicles on Thanksgiving, from 1,556 to 1,841, EVwire's tracker shows. Decoding the VINs, EVwire, TeslaNorth and Not a Tesla App count 1,107 Cybercabs and 734 Model Ys, the first time Cybercabs have passed 1,000. Not a Tesla App calls it the biggest one-day jump yet. Tesla hasn't commented; its last official figure, in October, was more than 300 Cybercabs running in Austin.

> Read it as: registered, not necessarily carrying riders. The register doesn't say which city gets the new cars.

Sources: [EVwire tracker](https://tracker.evwire.com/operators/tesla) · [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/26/tesla-now-has-more-than-1000-cybercabs-on-texas-robotaxi-roster/) · [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5095/teslas-cybercab-fleet-tops-1000-vehicles-in-texas) · [Sawyer Merritt on X](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2126037832611303689)

### 04. Tesla leads Germany's EV switchers, by a nose.
`Confirmed` · Tesla · Germany · EV switchers · 26 Nov 2026

Insurer HUK-COBURG's E-Barometer, published Thursday, says Tesla took 10.1% of its private customers' switches from combustion cars to new EVs in January to September 2026, putting it in first place, ahead of Škoda at 9.8% and BMW at 7.1%. Volkswagen fell from third last year to tenth. In eastern Germany Tesla's share was 16.4%. HUK insures about 14.5 million vehicles.

> Read it as: HUK's own customers, private cars only, not national sales. TeslAnt notes the lead has narrowed from 1.7 points after the first half to 0.3 now.

Sources: [HUK-COBURG release](https://www.presseportal.de/pm/7239/6398141) · [HUK results (PDF)](https://static.c.huk.de/content/dam/hukde/dokumente/ratgeber/ergebnisse-ebarometer-q3-2026.pdf) · [TeslAnt](https://teslant.com/de/news/huk-e-barometer-q3-2026-tesla-first-10-1-percent-skoda-9-8-2026-11)

### 05. Samsung's chairman gave Musk no special deal, WSJ account says.
`Reported / Rumor` · Musk · Samsung · Memory chips · 26 Nov 2026

The Wall Street Journal's piece on the memory-chip shortage carries a secondhand story: Capital Group portfolio manager Carl Kawaja said Musk told him in June that he'd offered Samsung chairman Lee Jae-yong a long-term chip purchase commitment. "We get that offer from everyone. What else can you do for us?" Lee replied, in Kawaja's telling. Musk then offered to guarantee financing for a new Samsung plant and got the same answer. Per the Journal, Capital Group and Samsung declined to comment and Tesla didn't respond.

> Read it as: one person's retelling of a private conversation. Not confirmed by Musk or Samsung.

Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/memory-shortage-war-rooms-musk-samsung-wsj) · [Yonhap (Korean)](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20261126188000009) · [VLSI Korea](https://www.vlsi.kr/en/wsj-memory-dash-musk-samsung-en/)

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. Relativity Space eyes 239 acres at Kennedy.
`Reported` · Space Coast · Launch · 23–24 Nov 2026

FLORIDA TODAY reports that Space Florida's investment committee on Monday recommended a 30-year sublease of about 239 acres at Kennedy Space Center, near the old shuttle runway, to a project code-named Forge, which the paper identifies as Relativity Space. The board is due to vote on 10 December. Relativity is converting Launch Complex 16 for its Terran R rocket; its first $565 million phase is projected to bring 590 jobs. The company didn't comment.

Sources: [FLORIDA TODAY / News-Journal](https://www.news-journalonline.com/story/tech/science/space/2026/11/24/relativity-space-eyeing-239-acres-at-nasa-kennedy-space-center-near-former-shuttle-landing-strip/92922930007/) · [Wisevoter](https://wisevoter.com/world/us/fl/2026/11/24/space-florida-relativity-space-kennedy-sublease)

### 02. The St. Johns at Cocoa: still in major flood.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Weather · 27 Nov 2026

The NOAA gauge on the St. Johns River near Cocoa read 18.28 feet at 2:15 AM ET today, down about a tenth of a foot from yesterday's 18.39 and still above the 17-foot major flood mark (flood stage is 16). The forecast holds near 18.3 feet this morning. Check Florida 511 before driving in Brevard, and never drive through floodwater.

Sources: [NWS/NOAA Cocoa gauge](https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/cocf1)

### 03. Zoox passes 1 million riders.
`Confirmed` · Robotaxis · Competition · 24–26 Nov 2026

Amazon's Zoox says more than 1 million people have ridden its robotaxi, built with no steering wheel or driver's seat, EVwire reports. Zoox didn't split the figure by city or say how many paid; it charges fares only in Las Vegas so far, and started employee rides in Atlanta and Santa Monica this month. Separately, The Verge reports Waymo has paused commercial freeway rides in all its U.S. markets after California's freeway ban.

Sources: [EVwire](https://evwire.com/p/zoox-one-million-riders-robotaxi-milestone) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/1042151/waymo-highway-ban-ai-safety-recalls)
