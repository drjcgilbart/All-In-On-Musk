# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Saturday, 14 Nov 2026 · Issue 003 · Times ET**

## The brief

- Starship Flight 15 made orbit and deployed all 26 Starlink V3s. The booster skipped its catch and splashed down. The ship's first-ever catch try is set for about 4:17 PM ET today.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Flight 15: ship in orbit, booster in the Gulf.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Starlink · 13–14 Nov 2026

Starship lifted off from Starbase at 5:47 PM ET Friday. Ascent and hot-staging went to plan. Ship 42 reached orbit at about T+26 minutes, the second time Starship has done so, and deployed all 26 Starlink V3 satellites. Space.com, citing SpaceX's mission outline, says the batch adds about a terabit of capacity, roughly what 10 Falcon 9 Starlink V2 launches add.

Booster 22 did not go for the tower. SpaceX called off the catch and brought it down in the Gulf of Mexico about 6.5 minutes after launch. As of Friday evening, SpaceX hadn't said why. It would have been the first catch of a Version 3 Super Heavy.

- **Today's catch try:** about 4:17 PM ET (3:17 PM Texas time), roughly 22.5 hours after liftoff. No upper stage has ever been caught.
- **Only if healthy:** SpaceX says the ship returns to the launch site only if the flight team clears reentry, landing-burn and tower-approach systems. Camera-equipped Starlinks were set to image its heatshield.
- **Bail-out options:** a final checkpoint about 20 hours 41 minutes in allows an ocean ending instead. A Gulf splashdown zone covers the final approach.

> What to watch: the deorbit burn about 21 hours 36 minutes in (around 3:23 PM ET). If it fires on a Starbase trajectory, the catch is on.

Sources: [SpaceX: Starship Flight 15](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-15) · [Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/spacex-starship-flight-15-chopsticks-double-catch-super-heavy-ship) · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/starship-flight-15-powers-to-orbit-setting-up-a-possible-catch-on-saturday/) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/science/spacex-attempt-first-starship-spacecraft-recovery-land-15th-flight-2026-11-13/)

### 02. Cybercab goes on sale. The subscription isn't optional.
`Confirmed` · Tesla · Autonomy · 13 Nov 2026

Tesla's @robotaxi account posted Friday that "select customers are now being invited to purchase Cybercab for personal use." The order page it shared lists the two-seater at $29,990 before taxes, a $1,000 non-refundable deposit, and estimated delivery in December 2026 in Austin.

The catch: a $299-a-month Autonomy Subscription is required, with the first month included. The fine print reads "Subscription and regular vehicle maintenance required to operate," and the invite email says it's "only available in supported service areas." There's no steering wheel or pedals, so the car can't be driven manually outside those zones.

Musk promised a Cybercab under $30,000 at its 2024 unveiling. Electrek's math: $3,588 a year in subscription, or about $47,600 over five years including the car. Not a Tesla App calls it the first look at what an unsupervised FSD subscription might cost.

> Read it as: invite-only and Austin-first. Tesla hasn't said how many cars are on offer.

Sources: [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/13/tesla-cybercab-personal-use-orders-299-subscription-geofence/) · [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5012/tesla-opens-orders-for-the-cybercab-for-personal-use-costs-29990-and-requires-a-299-monthly-subscription) · [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-cybercab-29990-december-deliveries/)

### 03. Texas' next AG promises a hard look at the merger.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Tesla · Legal · 12–13 Nov 2026

Nathan Johnson, Texas' attorney general-elect, told Bloomberg that "any merger of companies of that consequence warrants tremendous scrutiny." He added: "I have no qualms about examining his relationships to the state." He takes over from Ken Paxton in January.

Johnson didn't discuss deal terms and said his transition team hasn't been in touch with Musk: "if Elon wants to talk, we can talk." Tesla is based in Austin and SpaceX at Starbase. Neither company responded to USA Today.

Context: Bloomberg reported Thursday that merger talks are progressing, with a deal possibly announced as soon as December. Neither company has confirmed a deal.

Sources: [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/antitrust/texas-next-ag-vows-tremendous-scrutiny-of-tesla-spacex-deal) · [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/state/texas/2026/11/13/texas-next-attorney-general-says-spacex-tesla-merger-warrants-scrutiny/92758152007/)

### 04. The lineup gets shorter: one Cybertruck, one fewer Model Y.
`Reported` · Tesla · 13 Nov 2026

Electrek reports Tesla's U.S. configurator now sells only one Cybertruck: the tri-motor Cyberbeast at $109,990. The Dual Motor and Premium AWD trims are greyed out. Electrek puts the Cyberbeast up 57% from $69,990 in early August, after two price hikes in a week.

Not a Tesla App reports the $41,990 Model Y AWD is gone from the U.S. order page, and that Model Y Premium and Model Y L orders are sold out into 2027.

Background: Musk said on 6 Nov that Cybertruck production at Giga Texas would wind down to make room for more Cybercab output, and on 11 Nov pushed the end to late summer 2027 to clear the order backlog.

Sources: [Electrek: Cyberbeast only](https://electrek.co/2026/11/13/tesla-only-sells-cybertruck-cyberbeast-109990/) · [Not a Tesla App: Cyberbeast price](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4979/tesla-ends-cybertruck-premium-orders-hikes-cyberbeast-price)

### 05. Grok Imagine loses most of its team.
`Reported` · xAI · 13 Nov 2026

The Information reports, citing people familiar with the situation, that about 60 people on the Grok Imagine video and image team were laid off in the past week from the SpaceX Super Intelligence group. Only a handful remain to keep the product running.

The read in the coverage: resources are shifting toward coding tools and agents. The Information also cited benchmarks showing Grok Imagine trailing rival video models. Grok Imagine is still available. No company announcement has addressed the cuts.

Sources: [The Information (on X)](https://x.com/theinformation/status/2121279457084244122) · [AI Weekly summary](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/spacex-super-intelligence-cuts-60-from-grok-imagine-video-team)

### 06. Musk joins Trump's trip to Manila and Shenzhen.
`Reported` · Musk · Policy · 13 Nov 2026

The AP reports President Trump plans to take Musk with him next week to the Philippines and China, per administration officials who previewed the trip on a Friday call and asked not to be named. ### 01. Vulcan slips a day. Amazon Leo flies at 12:32 PM ET.
`Confirmed` · Space · Broadband · 14 Nov 2026

ULA scrubbed Friday's Vulcan Leo 1 attempt after a power interruption at an Air Liquide nitrogen plant. It now targets 12:32 PM ET today from Cape Canaveral with 40 Amazon Leo satellites, Amazon's biggest batch yet. It's Vulcan's return to flight after a February booster nozzle failure, and the first flight of the redesigned nozzle. Why it's here: Leo is Starlink's main U.S. rival, and the launch lands about four hours before Starship's catch try. Spaceflight Now counts nearly 400 Leo satellites in orbit against more than 11,000 Starlinks.

Sources: [Spaceflight Now](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/13/ulas-vulcan-rocket-plans-return-to-flight-with-launch-of-40-leo-satellites-for-amazon/) · [ULA: Vulcan Leo 1](https://www.ulalaunch.com/missions/next-launch/vulcan-leo-1) · [Amazon: LV-01](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/amazon-leo/amazon-leo-vulcan-satellites-launch)
