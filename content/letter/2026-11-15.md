## The brief

- SpaceX caught Ship 42 with the tower's chopstick arms at about 4:20 PM ET Saturday. It's the first time a Starship upper stage has come back from orbit and been caught.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Mechazilla catches Starship.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Starship · 14 Nov 2026

Ship 42 came back through the clouds over Starbase Saturday afternoon and settled into the launch tower's arms at about 4:20 PM ET, roughly 22.5 hours after Friday's liftoff. "Mechazilla catches Starship!" SpaceX posted at 4:21 PM ET. No upper stage had ever been caught before.

The ship made 14 laps of Earth. SpaceX said a single-Raptor deorbit burn at about 3:23 PM ET went to plan, and every team, from heat shield and flaps to the tower, polled go for the return. On the webcast, SpaceX's Dan Huot called it "the holy grail of spaceflight" and said, "We caught a Starship, and now everything changes."

It wasn't the full double catch. Booster 22 diverted to the Gulf on Friday and was destroyed. SpaceX's Kate Tice said early indications point to the boostback burn ending early on a low fuel mass alarm, after which booster guidance saw it was out of position and aborted the catch. The root cause is still under investigation.

- **Musk:** "I hugged ship 42 upon its return," he posted on X.
- **Why it matters:** SpaceX says catching the ship is what makes quick inspection, refurbishment and reflight possible. Super Heavy has been caught on three earlier flights.
- **Next:** SpaceX hasn't set a date for Flight 16. The AP says it expects to start Starship launches from Florida's Kennedy Space Center within the next few months.

> Read it as: half of full reuse, demonstrated from orbit. The booster's fuel alarm is the open question for the next try.

Sources: [SpaceX: Starship Flight 15](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-15/) · [SpaceX on X](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2121715216597827756) · [Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/spacex-chopsticks-catch-starship-upper-stage-flight-14) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/spacex-plans-to-try-first-ever-catch-of-starship-rocket-from-orbit.html) · [AP via Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/spacexs-starship-captured-by-launchpad-arms-in-dramatic-first)

### 02. All 26 new V3 satellites check in healthy.
`Confirmed` · Starlink · 14 Nov 2026

Michael Nicolls, who leads Starlink engineering, said Saturday that all 26 Starlink V3 satellites from Flight 15 are healthy, have been in contact since minutes after deployment, and began raising their orbits that morning.

That makes 52 V3s delivered across two Starship flights. Starlink rates each V3 at 1 terabit per second of downlink, about 10 times a V2. SpaceX's mission page says the new batch could start serving customers within a few weeks, after orbit raising and checkouts.

Sources: [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/14/starlink-v3-satellites-healthy-orbit-capacity-starship/) · [SpaceX: Starship Flight 15](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-15/)

### 03. Musk on the Cybercab fee: "too high."
`Confirmed` · Tesla · Autonomy · 14 Nov 2026

A day after Cybercab orders opened at $29,990 plus a required $299-a-month Autonomy Subscription, Musk replied on X that "the monthly fee is too high." He said it "should match the cost of Tesla [Full Self-Driving] and connectivity for other models, which is more like ~$115/month."

He also said Cybercab "will be available nationwide, as we receive regulatory approval." For now, Tesla AI chief Ashok Elluswamy said the car will run unsupervised "inside all the same geo locations as @robotaxi, just serving the owners' needs." TechCrunch reports buyers must accept delivery in one of a handful of Texas cities.

> Watch for: an updated price on the order page. Musk's post isn't a price change until Tesla makes one.

Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/tesla-opens-cybercab-pre-orders-for-personal-use-with-geo-restrictions-and-a-299-monthly-fee/) · [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/tesla-invites-select-customers-to-buy-cybercab-for-personal-use-5027212) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2316848/tesla-starts-taking-cybercab-orders-for-personal-use/)

### 04. Ukraine says Starshield is still capped. SpaceX says no.
`Reported` · Starlink · Starshield · Policy · 12–14 Nov 2026

Ukraine's top diplomat in Washington, Denys Sienik, told RFE/RL on 14 Nov that speed limits on Starshield, the military version of Starlink, are "effective" and "widespread" after five days of daily tests. President Zelenskyy says terminals in Russian-occupied territory are capped at about 161 km/h, too slow for interceptor drones chasing jet-powered Shaheds.

SpaceX denies it. "This is false. There has been no change," its media team told Ars Technica. Ukrainian officials told RFE/RL the company told them, "We are not imposing any restrictions," and pointed to technical issues. Musk has said Starshield limits are set by the U.S. military and, ultimately, the president. Trump has denied involvement.

Senators Jeanne Shaheen and Chris Coons have asked Musk to preserve related records and to reply by 30 Nov.

Sources: [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/ukraine-starshield-spacex-russia-missile-airstrikes/33898879.html) · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/11/ukraine-says-spacex-starshield-is-restricted-again-spacex-claims-it-isnt/) · [Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/11/13/starshield-still-operates-with-restrictions-in-ukraine-zelensky-says)

### 05. Grok's text watermark is now off by default.
`Confirmed` · xAI · Grok · 14 Nov 2026

SpaceXSI posted that text watermarking "is now off by default" in its API. Musk followed up Saturday: "Text watermarking with Grok is only on where required by law or requested by the user."

Coverage of the change says the invisible, statistical watermark had been planned as on-by-default, and it will still switch on automatically where a law such as the EU AI Act requires machine-readable labels.

Sources: [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2121644169462681818) · [SI Agent News](https://siagentnews.com/articles/xai-shifts-grok-text-watermarking-to-opt-in-model-d1e6ba.html)

`Reported` · SpaceX · Tesla · Markets · 14 Nov 2026

Bloomberg reports that a SpaceX-Tesla tie-up could hand SpaceX a backdoor into the S&P 500, months after it was denied swift entry to the index. Tesla is one of the benchmark's biggest members.

Bloomberg has reported that talks are progressing. Neither company has confirmed a deal or terms.

### 01. Vulcan scrubs again. Amazon Leo tries at 12:11 PM ET today.
`Confirmed` · Space · Broadband · 14–15 Nov 2026

ULA called off Saturday's Vulcan Leo 1 attempt after a network communication problem between ground equipment and the rocket needed a fix that would have run past the 29-minute window. That followed Friday's nitrogen-plant scrub. The new try is 12:11 PM ET today from Cape Canaveral, pending range approval, with 40 Amazon Leo satellites aboard; ULA says the rocket and payload are healthy. Why it's here: Leo is Starlink's main U.S. rival, and this is Vulcan's return to flight after a February booster nozzle failure.

Sources: [Spaceflight Now](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/13/ulas-vulcan-rocket-plans-return-to-flight-with-launch-of-40-leo-satellites-for-amazon/) · [FOX 35 Orlando](https://www.fox35orlando.com/news/ula-delays-vulcan-rocket-launch-due-communication-issue-officials) · [ULA: Vulcan Leo 1](https://www.ulalaunch.com/missions/next-launch/vulcan-leo-1)
