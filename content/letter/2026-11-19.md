# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Thursday, 19 Nov 2026 · Issue 008 · Times ET**

## The brief

- At DealBook, Musk said Tesla and SpaceX would "probably" be better together, but he shouldn't get Tesla pay awards just for a merger.
- He said Anthropic's most likely ask is an extra 10 gigawatts of SpaceX compute, and called Zelenskyy's Starshield claims "false."
- Terafab: Musk wants "hardcore engineers" and is targeting a first test chip around June 2027.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. "Would they be better together? Probably yes."
`Confirmed` · Tesla · SpaceX · DealBook · 18 Nov 2026

Musk joined Andrew Ross Sorkin by video from China at the New York Times DealBook Summit on Wednesday evening. On a Tesla-SpaceX merger: "You don't need them to be together, but would they be better together? I mean, probably yes." He said five years ago he'd have said no, and that AI, robotics and chip design changed the math. Any deal "require[s] shareholder votes, which may or may not succeed."

On his Tesla pay: "I don't think I should be compensated with any kind of award just for doing a merger. That would be unreasonable and not right." Yahoo Finance notes Tesla's 2025 package covers 423.7 million performance shares.

- **Anthropic:** SpaceX's compute deals can be ended on 3 to 6 months' notice, but Musk said Anthropic told him its "highest probability outcome" is wanting "an incremental 10 gigawatts of compute from SpaceX." Nobody there "set off my 'evil detector.'"
- **Phones:** Direct to Cell version 2 satellites start launching next year with, he said, about 40 times the bandwidth. A rooftop "micro cell" using SpaceX's 800 MHz spectrum goes into production "next year."
- **Cars:** Cybercab seats two, which he says covers about 90% of passenger miles. For families, Tesla plans an autonomous Model Y with no steering wheel or pedals.
- **Politics:** After the midterms, "I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money and not seeing much for it."
- **Nvidia:** Pushing Jensen Huang out was like cutting Michael Jordan mid-winning streak (see Beyond the Stack, item 03).

> Read it as: Musk's own words. No merger has been filed or announced.

Sources: [CNBC video](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/11/18/elon-musk-if-we-turned-off-starlink-the-entire-ukrainian-front-would-collapse-immediately.html) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/tesla-spacex-better-together-musk-015619735.html) · [Transcript (Singju Post)](https://singjupost.com/transcript-andrew-ross-sorkin-interviews-elon-musk-at-dealbook-summit-2026/)

### 02. Musk calls Zelenskyy's Starshield claim "false."
`Confirmed / Reported` · Starlink · Starshield · Ukraine · 17–18 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Zelenskyy posted on X: "The speed limit on Starshield in the temporarily occupied territories remains in place. We are discussing this with the American side." CNBC says drones moving about 100 mph or faster lose the link. At DealBook, Musk answered: "That's false ... Zelenskyy is not always truthful."

Musk's line: Starlink is civilian and capped at about the fastest road speed limit in Ukraine, roughly 80 mph; offensive use belongs on Starshield, which the War Department controls. "Do not ask me as a private individual to make kill decisions." He added that if Starlink were turned off, "the entire Ukrainian front would collapse immediately."

**Reported:** The Wall Street Journal reported on 20 Oct, citing U.S. officials, that Musk ordered an earlier slowdown. Musk has said limits on Starshield are set by the U.S. military "and ultimately POTUS."

Sources: [CNBC, Zelenskyy](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/ukraine-starshield-starlink-musk-spacex.html) · [CNBC, Musk](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/elon-musk-zelenskyy-starshield-ukraine.html) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1037448/elon-musk-ukraine-starlink-starshield-nyt-dealbook)

### 03. Terafab is hiring. First test chip around June 2027.
`Confirmed` · Terafab · 18 Nov 2026

Musk at 8:38 PM ET Wednesday: "Looking for hardcore engineers to join Terafab!" He was quoting a clip of himself, shared by fan account X Freeze, saying the research fab should "hopefully make our first test chip around June of next year," with volume production "towards the end of 2028, at least for phase one."

In the clip he says Terafab should ultimately reach 100 million square feet, "the largest building of any kind on Earth." It will start with fairly standard tools, then push suppliers for custom versions, and "in some cases, we may make our own machine."

> The clip doesn't say when or where it was recorded. Yahoo Finance reports Musk gave a similar May-or-June 2027 target last month.

Sources: [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2123229344936173890) · [Clip via X Freeze](https://x.com/XFreeze/status/2123218308027863259) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/tesla-spacex-better-together-musk-015619735.html)

## Outside the core: Beyond the Stack

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. ULA: Vulcan's engines were starved of fuel.
`Confirmed` · Space · Cape Canaveral · 18 Nov 2026

ULA's latest on Sunday's failure, posted at 11:00 AM ET Wednesday: "preliminary indications are that the low booster thrust was the result of inadequate fuel supply from the vehicle to the BE-4 engines, not from the performance of the engines." Not a final cause. ULA didn't say which part failed. Vulcan is grounded with no return date, and Spaceflight Now notes New Glenn is grounded too, leaving Amazon's Leo launches on Ariane 6 and Falcon 9.

Sources: [Spaceflight Now](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/18/vulcan-failure-investigation-focuses-on-inadequate-fuel-supply-to-booster-engines/) · [ULA](https://blog.ulalaunch.com/blog/vulcan-leo-1-updates)

### 02. Kennedy Space Center is building a coaster.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Visitor Complex · 17–18 Nov 2026

Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex unveiled Aero Test Lab, its first indoor roller coaster, at the IAAPA Expo in Orlando. Riders play NASA test engineers on Maurer Rides' SPIKE coaster, with scenarios running from subsonic to hypersonic. It's under construction and set to open in 2028.
