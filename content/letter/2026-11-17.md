# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Tuesday, 17 Nov 2026 · Issue 006 · Times ET**

## The brief

- Tesla will spend about €3 billion on a new Giga Berlin building for up to 1 million Cybercabs a year. Construction starts in the first half of 2027; production in 2029.
- Musk says trials of customer-owned cars on the Robotaxi network "may start" next month. Tesla's Q3 call had pointed to around Q2 2027.
- Musk says Transportation Secretary Duffy turned down free Starlinks for the FAA, days after an outage halted departures nationwide.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Cybercab gets a second home: Berlin.
`Confirmed` · Tesla · Cybercab · Giga Berlin · 16 Nov 2026

Tesla said Monday it will invest about €3 billion ($3.3 billion) in a new manufacturing building at Giga Berlin in Grünheide, built for up to 1 million Cybercabs a year. "Cybercab production is coming to Giga Berlin!" plant manager André Thierig posted. Construction is expected to start in the first half of 2027, with production scheduled for 2029.

Per Reuters via Investing.com, Tesla plans to apply for permits by the end of this year and hire several hundred more workers. The site built about 200,000 Model Ys last year against capacity of 500,000. Musk reposted the news overnight.

- **Context:** Cybercab started public rides in Austin in September. Tesla is also ending Cybertruck production in Texas in 2027, partly to make room for Cybercab, per TechCrunch.
- **Fleet count:** JPMorgan counts 1,306 registered Tesla robotaxis in Texas versus 1,321 for Waymo.

> Read it as: a 2029 plan, not a 2027 car. Permits come first.

Sources: [André Thierig on X](https://x.com/AndrThie/status/2122363982627389528) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/16/tesla-goes-all-in-on-the-cybercab-with-upcoming-e3b-berlin-gigafactory-expansion/) · [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/tesla-to-invest-3-billion-to-expand-berlin-factory-for-cybercab-93CH-5030628)

### 02. Your car in the fleet? "May start trials next month."
`Confirmed` · Tesla · Robotaxi · 16 Nov 2026

An Austin Uber driver who uses FSD posted that he keeps 49% to 63% of each fare and would rather send his Model Y to work for Robotaxi. Musk replied at 4:57 PM ET: "Coming soon. If things well, may start trials next month. Much better to manage a fleet of cars than have to drive just one car by yourself!"

Teslarati notes Tesla's AI leadership pointed to roughly Q2 2027 for customer cars joining the network on the October earnings call. December is also when the first personal Cybercabs are due in Austin.

**Also Monday:** Tesla's @robotaxi account posted, "You can now link your @XMoney to the Robotaxi app." TeslaNorth says the app shows a "Set As Default" option for X Money.

> Read it as: "if things go well" and "trials." No pay terms, cities or sign-up details yet.

Sources: [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2122449119570534680) · [@robotaxi on X](https://x.com/robotaxi/status/2122419204762820948) · [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-may-test-customer-owned-robotaxis-early-next-month-musk-says/) · [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/16/tesla-robotaxi-x-money-payment-integration/)

### 03. Musk: Duffy said no to free Starlinks.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Starlink · FAA · 16 Nov 2026

**Confirmed, his own post:** "As I was saying … Free Starlinks for FAA were offered to Sec Duffy, but he said they were not needed. As it turns out, they were," Musk wrote at 4:47 PM ET Monday, quoting his February 2025 post that the FAA's aging system was made by L3Harris.

**Reported:** The FAA halted departures nationwide on 13 Nov over communications problems at several air traffic control centers. Per Benzinga, Duffy has tied the problem to a router installation by contractor L3Harris and said the routers being replaced were about 25 years old. The exact cause is still under investigation.

> Duffy's side of the Starlink offer isn't in today's reporting. This is Musk's account.

Sources: [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2122446366354096479) · [Benzinga via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/elon-musk-says-sean-duffy-014354751.html)

### 04. Eight Cybercab crashes on file, and the fine print.
`Reported` · Tesla · Cybercab · Safety · 16 Nov 2026

Electrek counted eight Cybercab crash reports in NHTSA's public crash data two months into Austin rides. Four came in driverless service, none above 5 mph and none with injuries; two weren't Tesla's fault. The other four came in September with a test driver and driver-assist engaged, including a 53 mph highway crash in Philadelphia. Tesla redacted the narratives, so fault isn't public.

Business Insider read the Cybercab order and subscription agreements Tesla posted over the weekend: the $29,990 car "will not operate" without the $299-a-month Autonomy Subscription and runs only inside Robotaxi zones. Musk has said the fee should be closer to $115 a month.

> Watch for: NHTSA's open probe into how Tesla self-certified a car with no steering wheel or pedals.

Sources: [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/16/tesla-cybercab-crashes-speed-problem/) · [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/tesla-cybercab-autonomy-subscription-terms-conditions-remote-bricked-2026-11) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/teslas-cybercab-sales-to-the-public-is-early-validation-of-robotaxi-push-jpmorgan-says-192114709.html)

`Reported` · SpaceX · Markets · 16 Nov 2026

### 06. 5,000-gallon coolant spill at the Southaven site.
`Confirmed` · xAI · SpaceX · Southaven · 14–16 Nov 2026

SpaceX says a misconfigured hose a contractor attached to a mobile tank released about 5,000 gallons of propylene glycol, a cooling fluid, at its xAI site in Southaven, Mississippi, on Saturday. Some reached a drainage ditch. Mississippi's MDEQ says it stayed in the ditch and did not reach Horn Lake Creek, and it is testing water samples.

Young, Gifted & Green wants groundwater, soil and fish-tissue testing. A University of Memphis researcher told Action News 5 the response appeared to follow protocol.

Sources: [Action News 5, 17 Nov](https://www.actionnews5.com/2026/11/17/questions-remain-after-5000-gallon-chemical-spill-xais-southaven-site/) · [Action News 5, 15 Nov](https://www.actionnews5.com/2026/11/15/chemical-spill-xai-facility-southaven-reaches-drainage-ditch/)

### 01. Vulcan: early pointers, and a note from Musk.
`Reported` · Space · Cape Canaveral · 15–16 Nov 2026

ULA is still investigating Sunday's Vulcan failure off Cape Canaveral, which lost 40 Amazon Leo satellites. KeyBanc's Michael Leshock wrote that preliminary reports point to Vulcan's first stage, "raising the possibility of a problem" with Blue Origin's engines, per MarketWatch. ULA's own statement placed the anomaly about 10 minutes into flight, and Spaceflight Now tied it to the Centaur upper-stage burn, so the cause is not settled. Musk posted: "Sorry to hear this. Hope you are back to flight soon."

Sources: [MarketWatch via Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/2026111660/spacex-widens-its-lead-in-the-space-race-even-as-a-tech-selloff-weighs-on-its-stock) · [Spaceflight Now](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/15/ula-suffers-first-total-mission-failure-with-loss-of-vulcan-rocket-carrying-40-amazon-leo-satellites/)

### 02. Florida's last pitch for the Space Academy.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Policy · 16 Nov 2026

As the window closes for states bidding on the new U.S. Space Academy, which will train future Space Force officers, Sen. Ashley Moody is pushing for the Space Coast. "Why spend years and millions of dollars replicating what we've already built here?" she said. Moody's STARS Act and a House companion bill back the Florida bid; she says Space Coast launch activity is up more than 500% since 2016.

Sources: [Tampa Free Press](https://www.tampafp.com/florida-makes-final-pitch-to-land-the-new-u-s-space-academy/)
