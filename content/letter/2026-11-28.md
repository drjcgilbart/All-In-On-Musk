# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Saturday, 28 Nov 2026 · Issue 017 · Times ET**

## The brief

- Tesla closed at exactly $400 on Friday, up 5.2%, the day Germany's cartel office listed its filing to buy assets from EMAG, a maker of gear-cutting machines that also targets humanoid-robot parts.
- Musk overnight: Google "has expressed interest in renting more compute from us." SpaceX shares closed up 4.8% Friday.
- Musk on Grok Bot pricing: "We can't scale a negative gross margin business." A higher usage tier is coming, he says.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Tesla hits $400 as it files to buy EMAG assets.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · Optimus · Germany · TSLA · 23–27 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Germany's Federal Cartel Office (Bundeskartellamt) lists a filing dated 23 November, case B5-132/26, for "Tesla / EMAG-Gruppe (Vermögenserwerb)": an asset purchase, not necessarily the whole company. The listed markets are precision machining and electrochemical machining systems for metal parts, plus laser processing. No price, buyer entity or asset list is public. The office has one month from a complete filing to clear it, or five months if it opens a deeper review. Tesla stock closed at exactly $400.00 in Friday's shortened session, up $19.86 or 5.22%.

**Reported:** Reuters, via Automotive World, says Tesla declined to comment and EMAG didn't respond. EMAG, based in Salach near Stuttgart, makes turning, grinding and gear-cutting machines and sells equipment for the strain-wave and planetary gears used in humanoid-robot joints. Germany's tagesschau says a Tesla company, Tesla Machining Technologies Management, is already registered at EMAG's headquarters. Stuttgarter Nachrichten reports about 220 EMAG staff would move to Tesla. Wall Street Navigator notes the stock gapped up before the EMAG report crossed at 10:31 AM ET.

> Read it as: a deal under review, not a done deal. The filing doesn't mention Optimus; the robot link comes from EMAG's own robotics pitch and the reporting.

Sources: [Bundeskartellamt pending cases (German)](https://www.bundeskartellamt.de/DE/Fusionskontrolle/LaufendeVerfahren/laufendeverfahren_node.html) · [Automotive World / Reuters](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/tesla-files-plan-to-acquire-german-machine-maker-emag/) · [tagesschau / SWR (German)](https://www.tagesschau.de/inland/regional/badenwuerttemberg/swr-tesla-uebernimmt-maschinenbauer-emag-spezialmaschinen-fuer-humanoide-roboter-100.html) · [Wall Street Navigator](https://wallstreetnavigator.com/news/tesla-gains-5-22-to-400-as-emag-deal-report-follows-early-gap-higher) · [TSLA price history](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tsla/history/)

### 02. Google wants more SpaceX compute, Musk says.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · AI compute · Google · SPCX · 27–28 Nov 2026

Musk at 1:52 AM ET today, replying on X: "Not sure who will buy more, but Google has expressed interest in renting more compute from us." Nine minutes later he added that rental prices for "large, coherent blocks of compute configured for high-bandwidth training" are rising, "sometimes very significantly," while prices for under about 5,000 GPUs are falling. "Overall, the average rental price per GPU for SpaceX compute is rising."

SpaceX's SEC filing describes the existing deal: a 5 June cloud agreement under which Google pays $920 million a month from October 2026 through June 2029 for about 110,000 Nvidia GPUs and related hardware. SpaceX shares closed Friday at $133.59, up 4.83%.

> Read it as: interest, not a contract. No new deal, price or timeline has been announced.

Sources: [Musk on X, Google](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126569877221621795) · [Musk on X, GPU prices](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126572259896623544) · [SpaceX SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026041150/spacexagreementfwp.htm) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/google-lease-more-compute-spacex-musk/) · [SPCX price history](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/spcx/history/)

### 03. Grok Bot: no negative margins, and a bigger tier.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Grok Bot · Pricing · 27 Nov 2026

Replying at 3:29 PM ET Friday to a user who called Grok Bot's usage limits "still very bad," Musk wrote: "We can't scale a negative gross margin business. That's just impossible." If Grok Bots deliver value "on par with having excellent employees," he said, "the monthly cost can be quite high," but making upgrades complicated is "a mistake we've made before" that needs fixing. At 4:09 PM, to a user asking for a $2,000 to $5,000 a month tier: "Noted, will implement." At 10:36 PM he called it a "good idea" for Grok Bot to point out auto top-up credits when users near their limit.

> Read it as: no new tier, price or date has been announced. His usage tip from Friday: give each bot a specialized role and let a manager bot coordinate.

Sources: [Musk on X, margins](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126413161607692773) · [Musk on X, new tier](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126423243657822534) · [Musk on X, top-up](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126520586419552495) · [Musk on X, specialized bots](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126413886576590925)

### 04. Point at the screen, the car goes there.
`Confirmed / Rumor` · Tesla · FSD v15 · Reality View · 27 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Musk at 10:06 PM ET Friday: "The ability to point at a spot on the screen in reality view and have the car go there is coming out in a point release in a week or two." Reality View is the bird's-eye rendering that arrived with FSD (Supervised) v15.

**Rumor:** Sawyer Merritt guesses the update will also let drivers pick a parking spot. Musk replied "Yeah," without detail.

> Read it as: a Musk timeline. Point releases often reach a few cars first.

Sources: [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126513022080631259) · [Sawyer Merritt on X](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2126514029854351681) · [Musk reply](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126514569174802803)

### 05. Starship stacking in under five minutes, Musk says.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Starship · Starbase · 27 Nov 2026

Quoting a time-lapse of Ship 42 being stacked onto Booster 22 at Starbase, where the main lift alone takes about 30 minutes, Musk posted at 2:56 PM ET Friday: "This will be fully automated and sped up to be <5 mins." He also reshared footage of the Flight 15 ship catch twice: "Beautiful engineering" at 2:30 PM, and "Still hard to believe that this actually happened" at 11:53 PM, on a fan's edit syncing onboard, tower and ground views.

> Read it as: a goal, with no date. Fast stacking matters for the high launch rates SpaceX wants from Starbase.

Sources: [Musk on X, stacking](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126404709628129388) · [Musk on X, catch](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126398247552037042) · [Musk on X, hard to believe](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2126539857581511160)

### 06. Mexico wants a say in Starbase's flight rate.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Starbase · Mexico · 26–27 Nov 2026

Mexico's President Claudia Sheinbaum said at her Thursday morning news conference: "What we are seeking, through the embassy, is a meeting between US and Mexican airspace authorities so that permission for the use of Mexican airspace can be established, how often it would be used, and, if necessary, the relocation of the launch site." She cited SpaceX's goal of up to 400 flights a year and said talks should be government to government, not with SpaceX. Separately, Mexico's environmental agency Profepa says it recovered about 2 kg of suspected debris on Playa Bagdad after Flight 15. SpaceX said last week that very little debris had turned up and it wasn't clearly from SpaceX.

> Read it as: a request for talks. No meeting date, and no move to stop launches.

Sources: [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/mexico-seeks-talks-with-us-over-spacex-launches/4139346) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/26/mexico-seeks-us-talks-about-suspected-spacex-debris) · [Mexico News Daily](https://mexiconewsdaily.com/news/sheinbaum-press-us-spacex-launches-after-starship-debris-hits-tamaulipas-beaches/) · [El Universal (Spanish)](https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/nacion/sheinbaum-pide-reunion-con-eu-para-regular-vuelos-de-spacex-en-espacio-aereo-mexicano-busca-coordinacion-bilateral/)

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. The St. Johns at Cocoa: still major. FEMA aid is open.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Weather · 27–28 Nov 2026

The NOAA gauge on the St. Johns River near Cocoa read 18.18 feet at 2:15 AM ET today, down a tenth of a foot from yesterday's 18.28 and still above the 17-foot major flood mark. The forecast holds near 18.1 feet through Sunday. Brevard residents hit by the flooding can now apply for FEMA Individual Assistance at DisasterAssistance.gov, in the FEMA app or at 800-621-3362, 7 AM to 10 PM. If you have insurance, file that claim first. Never drive through floodwater.

Sources: [NWS/NOAA Cocoa gauge](https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/cocf1) · [WFTV](https://www.wftv.com/news/local/brevard-county-residents-can-now-apply-federal-disaster-assistance/ZWQ7T2B66VFHNGQBOW27UOAHTY/) · [WFIT](https://www.wfit.org/podcast/local-state-news/2026-11-27/wfit-local-state-news-november-27-2026-pm)

### 03. Amazon and Google chips post cheaper inference.
`Confirmed / Reported` · AI chips · Competition · 25–27 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** SemiAnalysis says Google's TPU and Amazon's Trainium are now on its InferenceX and AgentX benchmarks, at "1.2x to 2.5x the performance per dollar of NVIDIA GPUs." Musk, quoting a post about it at 8:40 PM ET Friday: "Super intelligent AI eliminates any company's software advantage."

**Reported:** Automater's breakdown says most of Trainium3's 2.28x lead over Nvidia's GB300 comes from SemiAnalysis's lower cost estimate, with throughput only about 4% higher, and the results are a preview on one model.
