**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. SpaceX walks away from $738 million in broadband money.
`Confirmed / Reported` · SpaceX · Starlink · BEAD · Broadband · 23–24 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** The National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA) published a "Model Zero Outlay Agreement" for the federal BEAD broadband program at 5:00 PM ET Monday. It is for a low-Earth-orbit provider that declines its BEAD subsidies but still serves the locations it won. The provider must deliver at least 100 Mbps down and 20 Mbps up, keep 95% of latency tests at or below 100 milliseconds, supply the dish and other equipment at no extra cost (shipping aside), offer a low-cost plan, pass performance testing and file twice-yearly reports. The agreement obligates $0 in federal funds. States that don't sign it must find another provider at a comparable cost.

**Reported:** Broadband Breakfast, citing a senior NTIA official, says SpaceX proposed the idea after struggling with differing contract terms across states, including financial reporting rules and default penalties, and is ready to sign. It puts the forgone awards at about $738 million. The official said contracts some states had already signed with SpaceX are no longer on the table.

**Confirmed:** Musk at 6:00 PM ET Tuesday, quoting Sawyer Merritt's post on the deal: "Starlink".

> Read it as: the model agreement is NTIA's own document. SpaceX's role and the $738 million figure come from NTIA's official via Broadband Breakfast and Musk's quote post; SpaceX hasn't published its own statement. Each state still decides whether to sign.

Sources: [NTIA BroadbandUSA](https://broadbandusa.ntia.gov/waivers-policies/BEAD_Model_Zero_Outlay_Agreement) · [Model agreement (PDF)](https://broadbandusa.ntia.gov/sites/default/files/2026-11/BEAD_Model_Zero_Outlay_Agreement.pdf) · [Broadband Breakfast](https://broadbandbreakfast.com/spacex-declines-bead-winnings-wants-to-serve-locations-at-no-cost/) · [PCMag](https://au.pcmag.com/networking/121048/spacex-to-feds-well-offer-starlink-for-free-in-bead-areas-if-you-drop-oversight) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2125363942977597554)

### 02. Robotaxi counter passes 6 million miles.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · Robotaxi · Cybercab · 24–25 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Tesla's Robotaxi safety page showed more than 6 million estimated autonomous miles on Tuesday; TeslaNorth logged 6,020,733. Tesla says the live number is an estimate from the fleet's current pace. Its latest measured report covers 3.1 million miles from 22 January through 18 October, with zero people injured in collisions where the Robotaxi made first contact, by Tesla's definition.

**Reported:** The EVwire tracker of Texas DMV records shows 1,556 Tesla vehicles registered for automated operation, 822 Cybercabs and 734 Model Ys, against Waymo's 1,405. Sawyer Merritt reports riders can now extend their pickup wait to up to 15 minutes.

> Read it as: 6 million is Tesla's estimate, not an audited total. Registrations count vehicles authorized in Texas, not cars on the road at once.

Sources: [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/24/tesla-robotaxi-surpasses-6-million-miles-on-its-live-counter/) · [EVwire Texas tracker](https://tracker.evwire.com/) · [EVwire, Tesla fleet mix](https://tracker.evwire.com/charts/tesla-mix) · [Sawyer Merritt on X](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2125218065965355341)

### 03. X wins a UK judgment over creator-payout fraud.
`Confirmed` · X · Courts · Creator payouts · 24 Nov 2026

X general counsel James Burnham at 1:16 PM ET Tuesday: "In September, @X sued people who abused Creator Revenue Sharing by operating a network of accounts to manipulate engagement. The UK High Court has now entered a judgment against two of them." Crypto.news and The Crypto Times describe it as a default judgment dated 20 November after the two defendants didn't respond, and say damages are still to be set. X's original claim alleged the network drew more than £207,000 in payouts. Musk quoted it at 7:18 PM ET: "Don't mess with X".

> Read it as: the £207,000 is X's allegation, not an award. Damages haven't been decided.

Sources: [Burnham on X](https://x.com/i/status/2125292603331502105) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2125383610266157090) · [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/24/x-says-uk-court-entered-judgment-in-creator-payout-case/) · [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/x-wins-bitcoin-creator-payout-fraud-lawsuit/) · [The Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/11/25/x-wins-uk-high-court-default-judgment-in-207k-bitcoin-creator-payout-case/)

### 04. Falcon 9 flies USSF-266 from California.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Falcon 9 · National security · 24 Nov 2026

Falcon 9 lifted off at 11:09 AM ET Tuesday from pad 4E at Vandenberg Space Force Base with the USSF-266 mission for the U.S. Space Force, and SpaceX confirmed at 4:33 PM ET that it reached orbit. The first stage landed on the Of Course I Still Love You droneship. It was SpaceX's fourth national-security launch from California in eight days, after USSF-317 (20 Nov) and NROL-136 (22 Nov). Next on the list: USSF-380 from California on Saturday night, and NG-25 to the space station from Florida on Sunday.

> Read it as: the payload is classified; SpaceX gave no details.

Sources: [SpaceX on X, orbit](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2125342179216261600) · [SpaceX on X, landing](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2125263043244290435) · [SpaceX launches](https://www.spacex.com/launches/ussf-266)

### 05. Musk confirms $500,000 for Austin foster youth.
`Confirmed` · Musk Foundation · Austin · 18–24 Nov 2026

The City of Austin said on 18 November it secured $500,000 in matching funds from the Musk Foundation, unlocking $500,000 the city had set aside, for $1 million to help up to 100 young adults leaving foster care find housing and support services. The money pairs with federal Foster Youth to Independence vouchers, which cover up to 36 months of rent. Musk, quoting a post on the gift at 7:11 PM ET Tuesday: "The news is accurate".

Sources: [City of Austin](https://www.austintexas.gov/homeless-strategies/news/city-austin-secures-matching-funds-expand-housing-assistance-former-foster) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2125381945672761599)

## Outside the core: Beyond the Stack

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. Stoke's Nova booster reaches the Cape.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Launch · 22–24 Nov 2026

Stoke Space said Tuesday its Nova Pathfinder first stage arrived at Space Launch Complex 14 at Cape Canaveral on 22 November, the first rocket at that pad in 60 years; John Glenn launched from it in 1962. The road trip from Washington state took 17 days. Stoke is aiming for a first launch of the fully reusable rocket in early 2027. One more company getting ready to fly from the Cape alongside SpaceX.

Sources: [Stoke Space](https://www.stokespace.com/news/pathfinder-arrives-at-launch-site)

### 02. The St. Johns at Cocoa: still above flood stage.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Weather · 25 Nov 2026

The NOAA gauge on the St. Johns River near Cocoa read 18.42 feet at 2:15 AM ET today, down about an inch from yesterday's 18.53 and still well above the 16-foot flood stage. Check Florida 511 before driving in Brevard, and never drive through floodwater.

Sources: [NWS/NOAA Cocoa gauge](https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/cocf1)

### 03. Nuro, Lucid and Uber pull the safety drivers.
`Confirmed` · Robotaxis · Competition · 24 Nov 2026

Nuro, Lucid and Uber said Tuesday they've started fully driverless testing of Lucid Gravity robotaxis on public roads in Palo Alto, Mountain View, Los Altos and Sunnyvale. Employee rides come next, with a public Bay Area launch on Uber expected in early 2027. Tesla's own Bay Area service still uses a safety driver.

Sources: [Nuro](https://www.nuro.ai/blog/nuro-lucid-uber-begin-driverless-testing-on-public-roads) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/uber-and-nuro-begin-driverless-testing-of-lucid-robotaxis-ahead-of-2027-launch/)
