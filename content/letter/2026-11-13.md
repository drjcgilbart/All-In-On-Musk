## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Flight 15: tonight's the night, on paper.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · 13 Nov 2026

SpaceX's Flight 15 page still targets today, Friday 13 Nov, with a 75-minute window opening at 5:45 PM ET (4:45 PM CT). As of this morning the page still reads "pending regulatory approval." No FAA license action for the flight had been announced. The webcast starts about 30 minutes before liftoff on spacex.com and @SpaceX.

- **Booster 22** aims for a tower catch at about T+6:35. SpaceX ran a "squeeze test" on 10 Nov, with the booster held by the tower arms at catch height.
- **Ship 42** deploys 26 Starlink V3 satellites, then coasts for about 14 orbits. The catch attempt comes at about T+22:32. On an on-time launch, that's near 4:17 PM ET Saturday.
- Three of the 26 satellites carry cameras to scan Starship's heatshield before the ship commits to coming home. If the flight team doesn't clear reentry and landing systems, the ship ends in the ocean instead.
- Heatshield changes from Flight 14 carry over, plus a large area of new curved tiles meant to cut heating in the gaps.

> What to watch: the license first, then weather and pad readiness. A slip of a day or two would not be unusual.

Sources: [SpaceX: Starship Flight 15](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-15) · [SpaceX: Returning Starship to the launch site](https://www.spacex.com/updates/starship-catch) · [Space.com: booster squeeze test](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/spacex-moves-starship-flight-15-booster-to-launch-pad-for-squeeze-test-ahead-of-epic-double-catch-attempt-photos)

### 02. Merger talks move to price and control.
`Reported` · SpaceX · Tesla · 12 Nov 2026

Bloomberg reported Thursday, citing people familiar with the matter, that SpaceX and Tesla merger talks are progressing and a deal could be announced as soon as December. Early 2027 is also being discussed. Reuters relayed the report; neither company commented.

Electrek, summarizing Bloomberg, says the open terms include how many SpaceX shares Tesla holders would get, which points to SpaceX absorbing Tesla. Ring-fencing Tesla's China business is described as a main obstacle. Musk called a merger a "good idea" last month, and both boards have formed special committees.

Dates to note: Tesla's annual shareholder meeting is 3 Dec. The new Congress takes over in January.

> Read it as: talks, not a deal. No terms, price or filing yet.

Sources: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/spacex-tesla-merger-talks-progress-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-11-12/) · [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/12/spacex-tesla-merger-announcement-december-report/) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1032350/spacex-tesla-mega-merger-could-come-as-soon-as-december)

### 03. $74B for two compute campuses.
`Confirmed / Reported` · SpaceX · AI · 12 Nov 2026

SpaceX said Thursday it plans to invest more than $74 billion in two AI supercompute campuses. About $24 billion goes to Starbase, Texas, on SpaceX-owned land with its own gas-fired power plant instead of grid power. About $50 billion goes to a campus in Cochise County, Arizona, which would use dry cooling with no planned industrial groundwater use.

SpaceX's job counts: more than 3,000 construction jobs and 500-plus permanent jobs in Texas; about 5,500 construction and 1,000 permanent in Arizona. It says the Arizona project could cut bills for local electric co-op members by up to 20%.

**Reported:** Reuters says the Arizona campus still needs county approval, faces a 2,400-signature petition against it, and has a public hearing in December. Starbase's city commission takes up a property tax abatement for the Texas site on 9 Dec. Tech Times says the Starbase plant's state air registration was still listed as pending as of 12 Nov.

Sources: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/spacex-invest-over-74-billion-supercompute-facilities-texas-arizona-2026-11-12/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/spacex-plans-74-billion-data-center-projects-in-arizona-texas) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330622/20261113/spacex-plans-74b-data-centers-starbase-gas-plant-nitrogen-oxide-cap-114-tons-under-federal-line.htm)

### 04. Grok Build's open-door problem.
`Reported` · xAI · 10–12 Nov 2026

Security firm Claroc says 35,578 of 37,571 live apps published with Grok Build (94.7%) opened for anyone with the link, no sign-in needed. Two dozen were built by SpaceXSI and X staff, including a confidential onboarding plan, a map of 594 sales accounts and ad proposals. Claroc says SpaceXSI took its own 24 apps offline after the report.

How they were found: each published app gets its own grok.me hostname, and those names show up in commercial DNS lookup databases. Claroc says the publish screen suggested "Anyone with the link" in the account it tested.

The company's side, via Ars Technica: "Apps are private by default and nothing goes public unless the creator chooses to share it." Claroc sells security tools for AI-built apps.

> If you've published a Grok Build app, check its sharing setting.

Sources: [Claroc research](https://blog.claroc.io/ghostshares-grok-build-apps/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/grok-build-apps-exposed-claroc-spacexsi)

### 05. Nonprofits sue to freeze frontier AI, SpaceXSI included.
`Confirmed` · xAI · Legal · 12 Nov 2026

Public Citizen, Irreplaceable Education Fund, Heat Initiative and The Alliance for Secure AI filed suit Thursday in San Francisco Superior Court against Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceXSI. They invoke California's Unfair Competition Law and ask the court to freeze development of the most advanced AI systems. Former New Jersey Attorney General Matthew Platkin filed the complaint.

It's a filing, not a ruling. No company response had been published as of this morning.

Sources: [Public Citizen press release](https://www.citizen.org/news/coalition-of-nonprofits-files-landmark-lawsuit-to-stop-development-of-advanced-ai-systems-at-anthropic-google-meta-openai-and-spacexsi-that-proliferate-bioweapons-and-cyberattacks/) · [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2549592/anthropic-openai-face-suit-to-halt-insane-ai-development)

### 06. Fifty Semis head for Chicago. Autosteer's exit gets a name.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · 11–12 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Tesla's @tesla_semi account posted Wednesday that "Chicago's first 50 Semis" are en route for delivery. Tesla didn't name the buyer. Electrek ties it to HMD Trucking's 50-truck order, which HMD's owner told MotorTrend is worth about $15 million. Tesla also opened a six-stall Semi Megacharger in Hodgkins, Illinois. Tesla doesn't report Semi production, so the ramp rate is still unknown.

**Reported:** Not a Tesla App, citing one source with knowledge of the decision, says Musk personally decided to drop Basic Autopilot (Autosteer) from new U.S. and Canadian cars in January to lift FSD subscriptions. Ten million active FSD subscriptions is one of the milestones in his 2025 pay package. Tesla reported 1.74 million FSD subscribers in Q3. Tesla hasn't commented on the report.

Sources: [Electrek: 50 Semis](https://electrek.co/2026/11/11/tesla-semi-delivery-50-trucks-chicago-hmd/) · [TeslaNorth: Hodgkins Megacharger](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/10/tesla-semi-megacharger-hodgkins-illinois/) · [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4988/exclusive-musk-cut-basic-autopilot-from-new-vehicles-to-boost-fsd-subscriptions)
