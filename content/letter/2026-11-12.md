## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Flight 15: two catches, one launch window.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · 12 Nov 2026

SpaceX is targeting Friday, 13 Nov, for Starship Flight 15 from Starbase. The 75-minute window opens at 5:45 PM ET (4:45 PM CT). SpaceX's own page still reads "pending regulatory approval." No FAA sign-off had been announced as of this morning.

- **Booster 22 (Super Heavy V3)** heads back for a tower catch about 6 min 35 s after liftoff. It is the first catch attempt for a V3 booster. Three V1 boosters were caught on earlier flights, and two of them flew again.
- **Ship 42** deploys 26 Starlink V3 satellites, flies about 14 orbits at roughly 275 km, then attempts the first-ever ship catch at Starbase about 22 h 32 min after liftoff. On an on-time launch, that lands near 4:17 PM ET Saturday.
- The ship comes home only if flight controllers clear the systems needed for reentry, the landing burn and the tower approach. If not, the mission ends over the ocean.
- SpaceX says each V3 satellite adds 1 Tbps of capacity. The batch of 26 equals about 10 Falcon 9 launches of V2 Minis.

> Why it matters: SpaceX says success would be "the first full recovery of an orbital launch system." That takes both catches. Neither has happened yet, and dates slip.

Sources: [SpaceX: Starship Flight 15](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-15) · [SpaceX: Returning Starship to the launch site](https://www.spacex.com/updates/starship-catch) · [Space.com live updates](https://www.space.com/news/live/spacex-starship-flight-15-live-updates-nov-11-2026-starship-first-double-landing-attempt)

### 02. Cybertruck gets a summer.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · 11 Nov 2026

Musk posted Wednesday that Cybertruck production at Giga Texas will continue "through the end of summer 2027" so Tesla can work through more of the order backlog. The schedule has now changed three times in five days: "around the middle of next year" on 6 Nov, "about 8 months" on 9 Nov, and end of summer on 11 Nov. The freed line space is earmarked for Cybercab.

His own read on the call: "This is not the smartest move from a profit-maximizing standpoint btw, but there must be some room in this world for sentimentality or what's the point?"

**Reported:** Electrek says Tesla lifted Cybertruck prices by up to 10% and paused base Dual Motor orders, putting the cheapest orderable truck at $92,490. TechCrunch cites industry estimates of about 71,000 sold since November 2023. Tesla does not break out Cybertruck sales.

Sources: [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tesla-extend-cybertruck-production-backlog/) · [CNBC, 6 Nov](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/tesla-cybertruck-texas-musk.html) · [TechCrunch, 9 Nov](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/musk-says-tesla-will-refund-some-cybertruck-deposits-defends-decision-to-end-production/) · [Electrek, 11 Nov](https://electrek.co/2026/11/11/tesla-extends-cybertruck-production-summer-2027-fan-backlash/)

### 03. Robotaxi drops the closing time.
`Confirmed` · Robotaxi · 11 Nov 2026

Tesla's @robotaxi account posted Wednesday night: "Robotaxi now runs 24/7. Every city we're available in." In late October the window was 6 AM to 2 AM. Tesla said on its October earnings call that it aimed to unlock 24/7 service before year-end, so this lands early. Musk's add-on: "Robotaxi works 168 hours a week."

What did not change: no new cities, no highway access, no wider boundaries. A ride still needs a pickup and drop-off inside the zone, and a free car.

Sources: [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/11/tesla-robotaxi-24-7-every-city/) · [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/travel/26/11/63177389/elon-musk-says-robotaxi-works-168-hours-a-week-as-tesla-highlights-24-7-operations-across-all-active-cities)

### 04. Anthropic's $120.5B is a ceiling, not a check.
`Confirmed / Rumor` · SpaceX · AI · 10–11 Nov 2026

**Confirmed, in the filing:** Anthropic's amended S-1, filed with the SEC on 10 Nov, says it agreed to spend up to $120.5 billion through 2029 on Nvidia GPU capacity from SpaceX.AI LLC (formerly X.AI LLC), a SpaceX subsidiary. That works out to about $3.1 billion a month once all capacity is delivered. After non-cancelable periods end in January and March 2027, either side can cancel on 90 days' written notice.

**Rumor:** Meeting notes posted to X on 11 Nov claim Anthropic told sell-side analysts it won't extend past mid-2027. The post names no meeting, host or speaker. Neither company has confirmed it.

**Confirmed, the reply:** Musk wrote, "We would be happy to take the compute back. No problem. But I think the opposite is going to happen."

**Reported:** SpaceX's quarterly report lists one unnamed customer at 34.5% of Q3 revenue. Bloomberg, citing one person, says it is Anthropic. The filing names no one.

> Read it as: a cap with an exit ramp. Not a guaranteed $120.5B, and not a cancellation.

Sources: [SEC: Anthropic S-1/A (Amendment 2)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm) · [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/11/musk-anthropic-spacex-take-compute-back/) · [Tenbrief source check](https://tenbrief.com/en/2026/11/12/anthropic-spacex-compute-contract/)

### 05. Mississippi keeps the Southaven turbine permit.
`Confirmed` · xAI · 10 Nov 2026

Mississippi's Environmental Quality Permit Board voted on 10 Nov to deny an appeal of the March air permit for 41 natural-gas turbines in Southaven. The permit holder is MZX Tech LLC, an affiliate of SpaceX's AI business, and the plant powers data centers on both sides of the state line, including Colossus 2. MDEQ said the petitioners presented "insufficient evidence" to support their claims.

The Southern Environmental Law Center, representing the NAACP, Safe and Sound Coalition and Young, Gifted & Green, says the permit leans on flawed pollution modeling and skips required offsets. **Reported:** DeSoto County News, citing the Daily Memphian, says the vote was not unanimous. Commissioner Andy Gipson voted no.

Still open: a separate federal Clean Air Act suit over turbines already running at the site, pending in the Northern District of Mississippi. Opponents could also ask a court to review the board's decision.

Sources: [Action News 5 (MDEQ statement)](https://www.actionnews5.com/2026/11/11/state-rejects-appeal-over-spacexais-air-permit-southaven-facility/) · [SELC press release](https://www.selc.org/press-release/mdeq-permit-board-doubles-down-on-unlawfully-flawed-permit-for-xais-massive-colossus-gas-plant/) · [DeSoto County News](https://desotocountynews.com/mississippi-news/spacexai-southaven-permit-upheld/)

### 06. Prufrock-5 starts digging in San Antonio.
`Confirmed` · The Boring Company · 10 Nov 2026

The Boring Company began excavating Morgan's Inclusion Tunnel on Tuesday. The nearly mile-long tunnel will link Morgan's Hotel and Morgan's Wonderland, with stops at Morgan's Wonder Care and the Multi-Assistance Center. ### 08. PRIME2 opens to stroke survivors.
`Confirmed` · Neuralink · 10 Nov 2026

Neuralink registered PRIME2 (NCT07929792) on ClinicalTrials.gov. It is an early feasibility study of the N1 implant, placed by the R1 robot, in adults with severe arm and hand weakness from spinal cord injury, ALS, or chronic ischemic stroke. Up to 10 participants at up to five U.S. sites. The University of Miami is listed as recruiting. The primary measure is serious adverse device effects through the 90-day visit.

The N1 is an investigational device. A registered trial is not a market approval, whatever a few headlines said this week. On Blindsight, Musk said on 30 Oct it is "ready from an engineering standpoint" and waiting on regulators. The FDA has announced no approval.

Sources: [ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT07929792](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07929792) · [Neura Pod](https://www.neurapod.com/blog/neuralink-prime2-study-stroke-survivors)

### 02. NHTSA proposes lighting rules for cars with no wheel.
`Confirmed` · Regulation · AVs · Comments due 25 Nov

NHTSA's proposed rule would limit manual-control requirements for turn signals, hazards and beam switching to vehicles that have manual driving controls. It would also allow optional cyan ADS marker lamps that show when an automated system is driving. That applies to wheel-less designs like Cybercab. It is a proposal, not a final rule. Docket NHTSA-2026-1519.

Sources: [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/26/2026-21794/federal-motor-vehicle-safety-standards-modernization-of-fmvss-no-108-to-accommodate-ads-equipped)

### 03. Nvidia fallout revives the chip-curb push.
`Reported` · AI chips · Policy · 12 Nov 2026

CNBC reports that allegations about Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's relationship with a woman reportedly recruited by Chinese intelligence are strengthening the push in Congress to add chip-export measures to this year's defense bill, including the AI Overwatch Act, the Chip Security Act and the MATCH Act. CNBC, citing The Information, says Commerce is finalizing a rule to block remote access to advanced chips through offshore data centers. President Trump is expected to meet Xi Jinping at APEC in Shenzhen on 18 and 19 Nov. Why it's here: SpaceX's AI compute runs on Nvidia GPUs, and Terafab would need the same tool supply chain that the MATCH Act targets.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-trump-jensen-chip-export-controls-china-xi-.html)
