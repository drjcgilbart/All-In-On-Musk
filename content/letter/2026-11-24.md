# Today in Musk World
*Unofficial Musk news desk. Inside Elon's World of Companies.*

**Tuesday, 24 Nov 2026 · Issue 013 · Times ET**

## The brief

- The New York Times reports Anthropic pays SpaceX about $3.1 billion a month for computing power and Google about $800 million, and that Musk has told staff the company is falling behind in AI. Musk's reply: "NYT is posting the obvious as 'news'."

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. SpaceX rents its compute to its AI rivals.
`Reported / Confirmed` · SpaceX · SpaceXSI · Compute · Grok · 23 Nov 2026

**Reported:** The New York Times reported Monday that Anthropic pays SpaceX about $3.1 billion a month for computing capacity and Google about $800 million, under deals that could run through 2029 but can be ended on 90 days' notice. It says SpaceX's AI unit, SpaceXSI, now brings in more than half of SpaceX's revenue. Citing three people, the Times says Musk told engineers the company is behind rivals in AI, and in August renamed an internal group chat "Sell compute because we can't compete." It quotes a message from him: "the only thing that matters for competitiveness is revenue per GPU, not benchmarks."

The Times also reports SpaceX shut down its AI companions project in September, moved engineers off the Digital Optimus AI-worker project onto Grok's core, and disbanded the 60-person team behind the Grok Imagine image and video tool.

**Confirmed:** Musk at 7:14 PM ET Monday, replying to a post that said the compute deals were known five months ago: "NYT is posting the obvious as 'news' 🙄". At 7:30 PM ET he reposted a clip of Nvidia's Jensen Huang saying Musk uses Nvidia chips three ways at once: training his own models, running them, and renting them out.

**Reported:** MLQ.ai, citing TeslaNorth, says Anthropic's October S-1 filing discloses up to $120.5 billion of spending with a SpaceX subsidiary through 2029, reaching about $3.1 billion a month once all capacity is delivered, which Anthropic expects in December.

> Read it as: the dollar figures and internal messages come from the Times' sources, and SpaceX hasn't published them. Musk didn't dispute the report; he said it wasn't news.

Sources: [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2125020128484131013) · [Investing.com on the NYT report](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/musks-ai-push-faces-a-dilemma-as-rivals-become-major-customers-5046067) · [Quartz](https://qz.com/spacex-leases-computing-power-anthropic-google-ai-112326) · [MLQ.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/anthropic-and-google-pay-musks-spacex-billions-a-month-for-compute-as-he-tells-staff-it-is-falling-behind-in-ai/) · [Musk repost, Huang clip](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2125024317327167853)

### 02. Five directors now steer the merger talks.
`Reported` · Tesla · SpaceX · Merger · 24 Nov 2026

**Reported:** The Financial Times reported early Tuesday that the two special committees weighing a Tesla-SpaceX combination are led by five independent directors: Robyn Denholm, Kathleen Wilson-Thompson and Jack Hartung from Tesla, and Roelof Botha and Randy Glein from SpaceX. Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs advise SpaceX's committee and Evercore advises Tesla's, building models for an all-stock deal and an exchange ratio, the FT says. People involved told the paper the talks are aiming at a possible year-end deadline but are not close to agreement.

Reuters, carrying the FT story, notes Bloomberg reported earlier this month that a deal could be announced as early as December. The FT says Tesla would need an extraordinary shareholder meeting, and approval from more than 50% of all possible votes.

> Read it as: both companies have confirmed the committees exist, in their October quarterly filings. The names, advisers and timing are the FT's reporting. Musk isn't part of the negotiations. No terms have been agreed.

Sources: [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/spacex-and-tesla-entrusts-five-directors-for-merger-talks-ft-reports-5047055) · [Investing.com, FT details](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/spacex-tesla-directors-negotiate-potential-35-trillion-merger-ft-reports-5047091) · [The Tech Signal, Tesla 10-Q](https://thetechsignal.co/2026/10/23/tesla-spacex-special-committee/)

### 03. Two states sue SpaceX over Grok images.
`Confirmed` · xAI · Grok · X · Courts · 22–23 Nov 2026

Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield filed in Multnomah County Circuit Court on Sunday evening, and Virginia Attorney General Jay Jones in Richmond Circuit Court on Monday morning. Both name SpaceX Super Intelligence Corp., SpaceX Super Intelligence LLC, X Corp. and SpaceX. They say Grok was marketed as safe, including a "spicy mode," while it could produce non-consensual sexualized images of real people and child sexual abuse material. "Weak safeguards were not a failure but a feature," both complaints say. They seek injunctions, civil penalties and disgorgement; Virginia also seeks restitution. CNBC says they are the first states to sue SpaceX over Grok.

**Response:** SpaceX's @Safety account at 8:10 AM ET Monday: "Protecting children is non-negotiable. Sexual content involving minors, whether real or AI-generated, is abhorrent. It has no place on X or in Grok." In a statement to CNBC it said it acts on accounts that try to break its safeguards and notifies law enforcement.

> Read it as: these are allegations in complaints. No court has ruled, and as of Monday morning CNBC said the filings were still pending acceptance.

Sources: [Virginia AG](https://www.oag.state.va.us/media-center/news-releases/jay-jones/3162-attorneys-general-jones-and-rayfield-file-suit-against-spacex-parent-company-of-x-xai-and-grok-over-creation-and-promotion-of-child-exploitation-and-non-consensual-images-on-platform) · [Oregon complaint (PDF)](https://www.doj.state.or.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ODOJ-Grok-Complaint-Final-Submitted-2026-11-22.pdf) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/oregon-virginia-spacex-grok-deepfake-lawsuits.html) · [@Safety on X](https://x.com/Safety/status/2124853016377397403)

### 06. Free Starlink Wi-Fi heads to Aer Lingus's European flights.
`Confirmed` · Starlink · Aviation · 23 Nov 2026

Aer Lingus said Monday it has begun fitting free Starlink Wi-Fi to its short-haul fleet, starting with an A320 in retro livery and already live on select European flights. It expects 29 short-haul aircraft equipped in early 2027, with Spain, Portugal and Morocco routes among the first. It says customers on its Starlink long-haul planes use an average of 1.8GB per connected device. Musk reposted the news twice.

Sources: [Aer Lingus](https://mediacentre.aerlingus.com/pressrelease/details/108/26607) · [Musk repost](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124924416249466906)

## Outside the core: Beyond the Stack

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. The St. Johns at Cocoa: barely moving.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Weather · 24 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** The NOAA gauge on the St. Johns River near Cocoa read 18.53 feet at 2:15 AM ET today, down only a few inches from Sunday's record crest of about 18.8 and still well above the 16-foot flood stage. NWS Melbourne said Sunday the river would decline very slowly through Major Flood Stage this week. Check Florida 511 before driving in Brevard, and never drive through floodwater.

Sources: [NWS/NOAA Cocoa gauge](https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/cocf1)

### 02. Jensen Huang is back at Nvidia. Musk: "It is done."
`Confirmed` · AI · Chips · Nvidia · 23 Nov 2026

Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director on Monday, 11 days after he resigned. The company said the board took input from "senior levels of the United States government" and is confident his return "poses no national security risk," and that the independent committee's investigation will go on. Reuters says shares closed up 31.5%. Musk, who uses Nvidia chips across his companies: "It is done" (9:28 AM ET), "The mistake has been corrected" (9:41 AM ET), and at 12:53 PM ET: "those who support you when the chips are down are your real friends."

Sources: [Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/3fec1b0a85a0/technology/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo-2026-11-23/) · [Musk on X, done](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124872664938602736) · [Musk on X, corrected](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124875939133751684) · [Musk on X, friends](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124924349710741798)
