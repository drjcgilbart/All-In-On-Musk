## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Tesla builds its own transformer.
`Confirmed` · Tesla · Energy · Powercore · 17 Nov 2026

Tesla's Megapack team unveiled Powercore at 11:00 AM ET Tuesday: "Solid state medium voltage transformer, inverter and breaker – reliable, safe and secure. 7.4 MW/9 MVA continuously at 34.5 kVac w/ grid-forming support for 10MVA of peak power. Made in America. Operating 2027." Musk followed at 11:46 AM ET: "Advanced solid state transformers at scale from Tesla."

Per Electrek and pv magazine USA, the spec sheet lists 98.86% peak efficiency, 800 to 2,000 V DC input, a warranty of up to 25 years and no flammable oil. The built-in solid-state breaker clears faults in about 100 microseconds. Tesla energy chief AJ Booth told pv magazine: "We can interrupt faults a thousand times faster than traditional equipment."

- **Who it's for:** battery storage (straight from Megapack DC to grid voltage), utility solar, and 800 V DC data centers.
- **Why it matters:** conventional transformer lead times have run as long as four years, per pv magazine.
- **Rival:** Electrek notes ex-Tesla CTO Drew Baglino's Heron Power targets volume production of its own solid-state transformer the same year.

> Read it as: specs and a 2027 date, not yet a price, a factory location or a first customer.

Sources: [@Tesla_Megapack on X](https://x.com/Tesla_Megapack/status/2122721563493744771) · [Musk on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2122733232894468308) · [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/17/tesla-powercore-solid-state-transformer-heron-link/) · [pv magazine USA](https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2026/11/17/exclusive-tesla-unveils-powercore-medium-voltage-power-electronics-platform-at-re-2026/)

### 02. Grok 4.8: "another week or two of polish."
`Confirmed` · xAI · Grok · 17 Nov 2026

### 04. Ramaphosa: Musk's "difficulty" keeps Starlink out.
`Reported` · Starlink · South Africa · 16–17 Nov 2026

South African President Cyril Ramaphosa told Semafor's Silicon Valley event on Monday that Musk's "difficulty in understanding" the country's ownership policy is the main thing keeping Starlink out. The law requires at least 30% of a licensed local operation to be owned by historically disadvantaged groups. Musk has called those rules "openly racist."

"When you want to come and operate here, you've got to subscribe to the policies of the country," Ramaphosa said. He said a meeting with Musk had been discussed but schedules didn't line up, and that Musk should be one to say, "I would like to contribute to the development of South Africa and give back to this country that made me who I am today."

> Watch for: business group Sakeliga's Pretoria High Court case, filed 9 Nov, which argues the regulator can accept equity-equivalent investment instead of a 30% stake. No hearing date yet.

Sources: [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/16/2026/musks-policy-difficulty-prevents-starlinks-south-africa-entry) · [IOL](https://iol.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-11-17-ramaphosa-elon-musk-owes-south-africa-a-contribution-to-its-development/) · [TechCentral](https://techcentral.co.za/sakeliga-icasa-court-equity-equivalent-malatsi-policy/288313/)

### 05. Dragon waits on weather. A Space Force launch Thursday.
`Confirmed` · SpaceX · Dragon · Falcon 9 · 17–18 Nov 2026

NASA and SpaceX stood down from Wednesday's undocking of the CRS-35 cargo Dragon because of bad weather at the splashdown sites off California, NASA said at 1:44 AM ET. The next chance is 12:05 PM ET Thursday, after a weather review at noon today. Dragon has been docked for 34 days.

Also Thursday: Falcon 9 is targeting USSF-317 from Vandenberg, with a four-hour window opening at 9:46 PM ET. It's the booster's 11th flight, landing on the droneship Of Course I Still Love You.

## Outside the core: Beyond the Stack

*Outside Musk's companies, but close enough to matter. Nearby first, then the wider picture, only when it counts.*

### 01. ULA: Vulcan's booster came up short.
`Confirmed` · Space · Cape Canaveral · 17 Nov 2026

ULA's first findings on Sunday's Vulcan failure, posted at 9:30 AM ET Tuesday: "early indications are that just after liftoff the launch vehicle experienced low booster performance," so stages separated too low and too slow to reach orbit. The Centaur upper stage and its RL10 engines worked as planned but couldn't make up the shortfall; the solid boosters look nominal. The FAA will oversee the investigation. No root cause yet. The flight lost 40 Amazon Leo satellites.

Sources: [ULA](https://blog.ulalaunch.com/blog/vulcan-leo-1-updates) · [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/ula-ceo-says-vulcan-failure-probe-focused-on-liquid-core-booster/)

### 02. Florida files its Space Academy bid.
`Confirmed` · Space Coast · Policy · 17 Nov 2026

Florida formally submitted its bid for the new U.S. Space Academy on Tuesday. The plan: an interim campus at UCF for 300 students from fall 2028, then a permanent 1,000-acre campus at Kennedy Space Center for at least 1,200 students by fall 2031, with groundbreaking possible in 2027. Space Florida says the state has already invested more than $680 million in space transportation infrastructure.

Sources: [Space Florida](https://www.spaceflorida.gov/news/florida-is-a-go-submits-official-bid-for-the-united-states-space-academy)

### 03. Waymo pulls off the freeway, everywhere.
`Confirmed` · Autonomy · Waymo · 17 Nov 2026

California's DMV barred Waymo's driverless cars from state freeways after three of them entered an active construction closure on U.S. 101, calling freeway operations "an imminent hazard to public safety." The order dates from 11 Nov but became public Tuesday. Waymo says it has paused freeway rides in all its markets while it evaluates; surface-street rides continue.

Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/waymo-suspends-freeway-rides-nationwide-after-california-dmv-order/)
