# Today in Musk World

## The brief

- Italy gave provisional approval to Tesla Assisted Driving, the supervised system once called FSD, on Friday. It's the first big EU market to say yes, at €99 a month.

**How we label:** `Confirmed` On the record: the company, a regulator, a filing, or the person's own post. `Reported` A named outlet's reporting that the company hasn't confirmed. `Rumor` Secondhand, unattributed or unverified. Not a done deal.

## The stack: Inside the companies

### 01. Italy says yes to Tesla Assisted Driving.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · Assisted Driving · Italy · 20 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Italy's transport minister, Matteo Salvini, at 6:26 AM ET Friday: after a technical feasibility check, his ministry gave "the green light" to provisional type-approval of Tesla Assisted Driving, getting ahead of the EU recognition process, which he said is still being defined. Tesla Europe followed with "Now approved in Italy" and "Rollout begins soon," plus a video of a car driving itself through Rome. Musk replied, per Reuters: "Thank you, much appreciated!"

**Reported:** Electrek says Italy is the 12th European country to allow the system and the first of the EU's big markets. The Dutch regulator approved it first, in April. Italy's 59 million people nearly double the share of Europeans who can legally use it, to close to 30%. An EU-wide vote isn't expected before December. Il Sole 24 Ore says it will cost €99 a month by subscription.

> Read it as: a national, provisional approval for a Level 2 system. The driver still has to supervise and stays responsible. Tesla hasn't given a rollout date or said which cars qualify.

Sources: [Salvini on X](https://x.com/matteosalvinimi/status/2123739830194512067) · [Reuters via Euronext](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/italy-gives-green-light-tesla-self-driving-system-ahead-eu-approval) · [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/20/tesla-fsd-italy-approval-assisted-driving/) · [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/autonomous-driving-mit-authorises-teslas-system-AJuwvJNC)

### 03. "Hola Argentina." Tesla opens, Milei on stage.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Tesla · Argentina · 20 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** Argentina's presidency says Milei spoke at Tesla's official launch at Arenas Studios in Buenos Aires' La Boca neighborhood Friday. ### 04. Free Starlink for flooded Brevard homes through 20 Dec.
`Confirmed` · Starlink · SpaceX · Space Coast · 20 Nov 2026

Starlink at 6:13 PM ET Friday: "For new and existing residential customers affected by flooding in Brevard County, Starlink service is free through December 20." ### 05. Lenders to SpaceX: keep our chips on the ground.
`Reported` · SpaceX · Finance · Markets · 19–20 Nov 2026

The Financial Times, republished by Ars Technica, says lenders in SpaceX's roughly $43 billion "Project Saturn" debt package, secured by Nvidia AI chips, tried to bar SpaceX from launching that collateral into orbit, because chips in space can't be repossessed. It's not clear the clause made the final documents. SpaceX has said the chips will be used on Earth, and Moody's says they'll sit in US data centers majority-owned by SpaceX.

IFR says $32.1 billion of bonds priced Thursday through an Apollo-owned trust that buys the chips and leases them to SpaceX for five years. The biggest piece, $25.2 billion, yields 8.259%.

**Markets:** TIKR says Tesla and SpaceX shares each fell about 8% Friday in a broad AI and growth-stock selloff. That happened even though HSBC raised Tesla to Hold with a $359 target. HSBC values Tesla at $507 a share if SpaceX buys it and $212 on its own.

Sources: [Ars Technica / FT](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/spacex-lenders-dont-want-elon-musk-launching-their-collateral-into-space/) · [IFR](https://www.ifre.com/bonds/2516476/yield-trumps-tech-jitters-as-apollo-prices-divisive-spacex-chip-financing) · [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/tesla-sinks-8-spacex-down-7-rivian-down-8-as-ai-sell-off-swamps-hsbc-upgrade)

### 06. Himes to Musk: explain the Starshield slowdown.
`Confirmed / Reported` · Starlink · Starshield · Congress · 20 Nov 2026

**Confirmed:** In a letter dated 20 Nov, Rep. Jim Himes, the top Democrat on the House Intelligence Committee, asked senior SpaceX officials to explain October's Starshield disruptions in Ukraine, and whether SpaceX made the changes on its own or a US official directed them. He says staff requests got "no substantive response." Until he gets answers, he wrote, it's "difficult for me to continue supporting the authorization of funding" for SpaceX-developed, government-owned systems.

**Reported:** CNN says Himes also sent a classified message to Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton. Himes becomes committee chairman in January. Musk said in October that Starshield limits "are decided by the US military and ultimately POTUS."

Sources: [Himes letter (PDF)](https://democrats-intelligence.house.gov/uploadedfiles/11.20.12_letter_from_hpsci_ranking_member_jim_himes_to_musk.pdf) · [CNN via KESQ](https://kesq.com/news/national-politics/cnn-us-politics/2026/11/20/top-democrat-to-trump-intel-chief-no-funding-for-musks-spacex-without-answers-on-ukraine-starshield-slowdown/) · [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/spacex-funding-threat-ukraine-satellite-dispute-12750525)

### 02. BepiColombo enters orbit around Mercury today.
`Confirmed` · Space · Mercury · 21 Nov 2026

After eight years in space, the ESA–JAXA BepiColombo spacecraft is set to enter orbit around Mercury today. Its two orbiters, ESA's MPO and Japan's Mio, are due to separate on 9–10 December, and science operations start in April 2027. It will be the first mission to orbit Mercury with two spacecraft at once.

Sources: [ESA](https://www.esa.int/articles/about-us/directorates/science/bepicolombo/latest-updates-bepicolombo-s-arrival-at-mercury)
